# White Balance Adjustment
White Balance: The scrub nurse should aim the lens at a white background (such as gauze, saline towel), and the circulating nurse should hold down the white balance switch (White Balance) on the camera for several seconds to make the image color closer to natural and real, or until "White Balance is OK" appears on the monitor screen, then release the switch. # Safety Operation of the Light Source Main Unit
Avoid turning on the unit immediately after shutting it down within a short period, such as less than 15 minutes, as this will further shorten the lifespan of the light bulb. Always pay attention to the display of the light bulb lifespan on the main unit. Prepare a spare light bulb when it exceeds the lifespan, and avoid burns when replacing the light bulb. The main unit generates high heat during operation, so ensure good ventilation. After the operation, wait until the automatic fan stops (cooling to no wind noise) before turning off the main power switch.

### Notes:

<!-- Slide number: 46 -->
# Safety Operation of the Fiber Optic Beam
Fiber optic beams are prohibited from being bent at small angles to avoid breaking the fiber optic fibers. Ensure a tight connection when connecting to the light source main unit to minimize light loss. High-intensity light sources produce high heat, so avoid long-term照射 on the same point. The light path fibers can penetrate through the surgical drape, causing severe burns to the skin. Turn off or do not open the cold light source switch before connecting the lens. Especially when the cold light source is not needed during the operation, turn off the light bulb in a timely manner.

# Safe Operation of the Pneumatic Abdominal Pump
Ensure that the high-pressure pipe is connected tightly to prevent gas leakage. The main unit is equipped with a pressure relief valve, so no additional pressure relief valve is needed between the main unit and the CO2 cylinder; otherwise, the main unit will alarm indicating insufficient cylinder pressure. Check the pressure display on the left side of the main unit. If it is green, the operation can proceed. A red light indicates insufficient cylinder pressure and a replacement is required immediately.

Red light indicates a fault in the pneumatic abdominal pump.

### Notes:

<!-- Slide number: 51 -->
# Safe Operation of the Pneumatic Abdominal Pump System
Foreign bodies entering the body: The abdominal catheter must be flushed with air before being connected to the abdominal pump's elbow connector to prevent foreign bodies from entering the body through the lumen. Hypothermia: Studies have shown that every 40-50L of CO2 can cause a decrease in body temperature of 0.3°C. There are now abdominal pump models on the market with constant heat functions, so temperature monitoring should be strengthened during surgery.

# New Approaches to Hospital Medical Equipment Management
Discovering truly modern equipment maintenance management should be based on the premise of preventive maintenance (PM) for medical instruments.

Abroad, medical equipment maintenance activities based on PM account for nearly 70%, while traditional maintenance (TP) occupies less than 30%. In China, the situation is exactly the opposite.

<!-- Slide number: 64 -->
# New Approaches to Hospital Medical Equipment Management
It is not enough to train in-house engineers; it is more important to improve the operational and management skills of front-line medical staff regarding medical instruments.

Due to the heavy workload and complex situations in front-line clinical departments, it is unrealistic to expect everyone to be proficient in the use and operation of the instruments, as well as to eliminate routine malfunctions.

This requires the cultivation of a device manager in each clinical department, who should have a basic awareness of preventive maintenance for medical instruments.

References
Chang Xiao Yi, Li Wan Zhou, Wu Zhi Quan.
New Approaches to Hospital Medical Equipment Management. Vol. 26, No. 05, 2011.
