Fine Management: Fine management refers to the use of various management methods to achieve precision and dataization in every link of the management process, improve the organization's execution and efficiency, and enhance the overall benefits of the organization.

# What is Fine Management
Fine management of surgical instruments in the operating room ensures scientific management of surgical instruments. It reduces the idleness and waste of instruments, extends the service life of instruments, and improves the economic efficiency for hospitals and departments.
