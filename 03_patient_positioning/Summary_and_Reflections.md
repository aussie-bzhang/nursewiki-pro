Overview of Spinal Cord Injury

Definition

   Refers to the injury of spinal cord structure and function caused by various pathogenic factors, resulting in motor, sensory, reflex and other disorders below the level of injury, causing the patient to lose part or all of their activity, self-care, and work abilities. It is a serious disease and one of the main objects of rehabilitation treatment.

Etiology

Traumatic (84%): Traffic accidents, high-altitude falls, sports injuries, etc.

Non-traumatic (16%): Pathological fractures of the spine, spinal inflammation, spinal vascular embolism, etc.

| Home Guidance: Avoid vigorous neck movements, prohibit excessive flexion, extension, and rotation, rotate along the axis, maintain the functional position of the neck. |
| Rehabilitation Training: Develop a rehabilitation training plan, wear a neck collar, active and passive exercise of the limbs, strengthen fine motor activity training, progress gradually. |

| Defecation Guidance: Persist in daily finger force stimulation training, abdominal circular and acupoint massage, artificial stool extraction or the use of a laxative if necessary. |
| Dietary Guidance: Develop a dietary plan, strengthen nutrition, develop regular eating habits. Eat more high-vitamin, high-protein, and coarse-fiber foods. |
| Medication Guidance: Take medication as prescribed after discharge; regularly return to the hospital for follow-up, seek medical attention immediately if abnormal symptoms occur. |
| Follow-up: Communicate with the patient and family before discharge, exchange contact information, keep in touch with the patient's condition at any time. |

## Summary and Reflections
### Nursing Summary
1. Applying the思路 of the entire clinical nursing process has allowed me to gain a more comprehensive and detailed understanding of the patient's condition. This integrated nursing with doctors has enabled effective treatment for patients, enhanced the doctor-patient relationship, and increased satisfaction.

2. Spinal cord injury is a very serious disease with a long course. We must pay attention to the feelings of both patients and their families, improve patient compliance, actively cooperate with treatment, and thus improve the quality of life for patients.

3. Through the implementation of holistic nursing, I have become more planned and predictive in my nursing work. By using critical thinking, I have taken proactive and personalized nursing measures for the patient's nursing issues, thereby enhancing my clinical nursing skills.

### Notes:

<!-- Slide number: 35 -->
## Summary and Reflections
### Reflections
1. Through the implementation of case management and multidisciplinary collaboration, I have learned a case nursing management model that is guided by nursing outcomes.

2. Management: 
   1. Bowel management: Small-volume, non-retentive enema; finger stimulation, pelvic floor muscle contraction exercises, to maximize the training of the patient's bowel function.
   2. Bladder management: Simple bladder volume and pressure measurement to dynamically understand the patient's bladder condition and determine the timing for catheter removal; develop a fluid intake plan, intermittent catheterization.
   3. Rehabilitation functional exercise schedule: Implementing collaboration between family members, doctors, and nurses to achieve effective functional exercise and improve the quality of life for patients.

3. Shortcomings: Early intervention in nursing issues for patients with severe diseases and long courses is necessary, as well as psychological counseling for both patients and their families.
