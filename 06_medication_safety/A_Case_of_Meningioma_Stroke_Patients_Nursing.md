**Meningiomas**
During the growth process, brain tumors produce a large number of structurally abnormal tumor vessels. The walls of these vessels are relatively weak compared to normal vessels, making them prone to rupture and hemorrhage, leading to stroke, known as tumor stroke.
Definition: Originating from the dura mater and the subdural space, meningiomas account for 19.2% of intracranial tumors, ranking second in intracranial tumors.
Age of Onset: Women are twice as likely as men, with the peak age at 45 years, and children are rare.
Site of Onset: 50% are located adjacent to the sagittal sinus, followed by the convex surface of the brain, the parietal sinus, and less commonly in the clivus, sellar结节, olfactory groove, cerebellopontine angle, and tentorium cerebelli. Growth within the ventricles is rare, and they can also be found in the extradural space and occasionally in other locations.

Laboratory Test Results Analysis
The occurrence of hypernatremia is due to post-injury neurotransmitter secretion disorder, under the action of inflammatory factors, leading to hypothalamic regulatory imbalance, causing renal sodium excretion disorder, resulting in elevated blood sodium and electrolyte imbalance.
Factors: 1. Insufficient fluid intake
       2. Use of large amounts of diuretics for dehydration and lowering intracranial pressure
       3. Head trauma, neurosurgical procedures, etc.

The white blood cell count remains elevated, and the patient shows no symptoms of fever, cough, etc. Chest X-ray examination shows no abnormalities, and urine routine values are normal. Considered to be postoperative stress-induced elevation.

Three-step method for calculating total calories

### Notes:
Here, I introduce the three-step method for calculating the total calories to be consumed daily. Daily required calories = ideal body weight × your own calorie level. Ideal body weight refers to the weight suitable for different heights according to scientific statistical data, the formula for ideal body weight is height (cm) - 105; you can also choose a calorie level according to your actual physical activity status.

<!-- Slide number: 25 -->
Calculate the total calories needed by Old Zhang every day

Ideal Body Weight: Height (cm) - 105

Old Zhang's ideal body weight = 158 - 105 = 53 kg
Old Zhang belongs to bedridden normal body type, choose 15~20 kcal per kilogram of body weight
Old Zhang's total daily calories = 53 × (15~20) = 795~1166 kcal

### Notes:
The ideal body weight can be calculated as 175-105=70 kilograms. Old Zhang has a normal body type and belongs to light physical activity, so the total calories per day should be 30×70=2100 kcal.

# Discharge Plan - Dietary Guidance
## Dietary Guidance
Foods with anti-brain tumor properties: wheat, Job's tears, water chestnuts, jellyfish, asparagus, etc.
Foods with protective effects on intracranial blood vessels: celery, shepherd's purse, chrysanthemum leaves, lotus stems, etc.
