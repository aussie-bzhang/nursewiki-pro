**Epidemiology of AECOPD:**

The 2017 Global Initiative for Chronic Obstructive Lung Disease (GOLD) report indicates that by 2020, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) will become the fifth leading cause of disease economic burden in the world. [2]

In 2006, the hospitalization mortality rate for AECOPD in the United States was 4.3%, with an average hospitalization cost of $9,545 per person. Domestic studies show that the average hospitalization cost for each AECOPD inpatient is as high as 11,598 yuan. AECOPD has a serious negative impact on the quality of life, disease progression, and socioeconomic burden of patients. [2] Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease Acute Exacerbation (AECOPD) Expert Group. International Journal of Respiratory Medicine. 2017;37(14):1041-1057.

### Notes:

<!-- Slide number: 5 -->

Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD), commonly known as chronic obstructive lung disease, is a common and frequently occurring disease that seriously endangers human health. COPD ranks fourth among the global causes of death, and it is expected to rise to third by 2020. [2]
Acute Exacerbation of Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (AECOPD) is defined as an acute deterioration of respiratory symptoms, leading to the need for additional treatment. Clinically, AECOPD is an acute onset process, where the respiratory system symptoms of COPD patients worsen acutely, beyond daily variation, and require a change in medication. [2]
Acute Exacerbation of Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (AECOPD) is an important factor in the death of COPD patients, having a serious negative impact on the quality of life, lung function, disease progression, and economy of patients. [2] Therefore, I have chosen this case as the subject of my writing, hoping to learn the relevant knowledge of this type of disease through individual case care, in order to guide my future clinical nursing practice.

### Summary and Reflection:

| The head-to-toe assessment method is suitable for assessing patients with acute exacerbation of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease. Before the assessment, it is necessary to fully understand the patient's medical history, current condition, auxiliary examinations, test results, vital signs, main medications, use of instruments, etc. Then, plan and perform a detailed head-to-toe assessment. After the assessment, summarize the patient's main nursing issues and potential issues by system, then formulate nursing goals and interventions, and finally make a comprehensive evaluation. Through the care of this patient, I think that in the care of patients with acute exacerbation of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, it is necessary to: closely observe changes in vital signs and ventilator parameters, strictly implement aseptic operation, closely monitor electrolyte levels, perform good nutritional assessment and skin care, and strictly prevent complications. |
