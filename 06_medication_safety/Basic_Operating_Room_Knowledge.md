# I. Use of Infusion. Purpose
1
Supplement water and electrolytes, prevent and correct imbalances in water, electrolytes, and acid-base balance

2
Increase blood volume, improve microcirculation, and maintain blood pressure

3
Administer drugs, detoxify, control infection, diuretic, and treat diseases

4
Supplement nutrition, provide calories, and promote tissue repair

# III. Use of Catheter

Observe for blood return in the catheter section, after blood return, lower the angle to 5~15° and advance slightly more to ensure the tip of the catheter enters the vascular cavity
Hold the catheter base with the left hand and the needle wing base with the right hand, completely withdraw the needle core, and activate the safety protection device
Rotate the needle wing and catheter base to the left and right to loosen the needle core to prevent upward and downward movement
Hold the catheter base with one hand and gently remove the needle protector cap vertically upwards
Fix the catheter base extension arm with the left hand and withdraw the needle core 2~3mm with the needle wing in the hand
Wash hands
Put on gloves
Assess the environment
Rotate the needle wing and catheter base to the left and right to loosen the needle core to prevent upward and downward movement
Discard the needle core with the activated protection device into the sharp waste collection box
Skin tacking, hold the needle wing and puncture the vein vertically at an angle of 15~30°
