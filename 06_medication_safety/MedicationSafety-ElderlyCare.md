Introduction -- Thoracic Duct
Introduction

The thoracic duct is the largest lymphatic vessel in the human body and the main route of the human lymphatic circulation!

60%-70% of the fat ingested by the human body enters the thoracic duct!

The thoracic duct contains albumin, globulin, fibrinogen, prothrombin, T lymphocytes, and other substances!

Introduction -- Chylothorax Definition
Chylothorax: Refers to the condition where chyle leaks into the pleural cavity due to rupture or obstruction of the thoracic duct caused by different reasons.

Introduction

Clinical Manifestations:

Dyspnea, respiratory distress, mediastinal shift, etc., which can lead to malnutrition due to excessive loss of fat, protein, and electrolytes, or immune function defects due to excessive loss of T lymphocytes.
