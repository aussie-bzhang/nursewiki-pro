# How to Prevent Pneumonia
The patient currently has factors that are prone to aspiration pneumonia, such as:
1. Previous smoking history, advanced age
2. Comorbidities (paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardia; hypertension, epilepsy)
3. Dysphagia
4. Weak cough
5. Long-term nasogastric tube placement, family members not managing position and nasogastric volume adequately
6. Oral hygiene
7. Nutritional deficiency

Improving these issues is necessary to prevent pneumonia

# Summary and Reflections
During the case management process, consolidate learned knowledge and search for evidence-based references to learn new knowledge.

Comprehensive nursing interventions can effectively control the occurrence of decubitus pneumonia in long-term bedridden patients with cerebral infarction.

The elderly often have multiple diseases coexisting. Although an acute disease may occur, a comprehensive assessment of all functional systems is still needed, taking into account chronic disease management and home management.
