护理问题和措施

1. 护理问题：发热、感染
   护理措施：给予抗生素治疗，监测体温变化，保持病室清洁，加强口腔护理，预防交叉感染。

2. 护理问题：褥疮
   护理措施：保持床单干燥、平整，定时翻身，使用气垫床，加强皮肤护理，预防压疮。

3. 护理问题：电解质紊乱
   护理措施：监测电解质变化，调整饮食，必要时给予补液治疗。

4. 护理问题：营养不足
   护理措施：给予营养支持，鼓励患者进食，必要时给予鼻饲。

5. 护理问题：认知功能障碍
   护理措施：加强心理护理，鼓励患者参与活动，预防跌倒。

<!-- Slide number: 11 -->
出院教育

1. 告知患者及家属关于菌血症、褥疮、电解质紊乱等疾病的预防知识。
2. 指导患者正确使用抗生素，注意观察药物不良反应。
3. 强调营养的重要性，指导患者合理饮食。
4. 告知患者定期复查，如有不适及时就诊。

P8: Risks of Deep Vein Thrombosis Formation
| Date | Basis | Nursing Measures |  | Effect Evaluation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| October 16 | Patient has been bedridden for a long time, weak, and lacks activity. Autar score: 9 | October 16-30 | 1. Consume high-protein, high-vitamin, and high-fiber foods to ensure adequate nutrient intake, eat more fresh vegetables and fruits, and black fungus, and avoid greasy, spicy foods. 2. Instruct the family to perform abdominal circular massage daily to maintain bowel movement, keep daily fluid intake at 1,000 ml or more, ensure sufficient fluid volume, and prevent blood hypercoagulability. 3. Closely observe changes in the patient's condition, vital signs, and observe the skin color, temperature, and dorsalis pedis pulse of the affected limb every day. A difference of more than 1 cm between the two sides suggests thrombosis[1]. 4. Perform lower limb muscle massage daily from distal to proximal, 15-20 minutes per session, 4-6 times/d[2], and avoid pressure on the popliteal fossa to promote blood return. Massage both lower limbs to prevent muscle atrophy, instruct ankle pump exercises, gentle movements, 2 times/day, each for 3-5 minutes. 5. Perform extension and flexion exercises of the knee and hip joints, gradually increasing the range of motion, and gradually increasing the force with gentle techniques, trying to reach the maximum range of motion of the joints within the patient's tolerance. 6. The incidence of lower limb venous thrombosis is 3 times higher than that of the upper limb. Avoid unnecessary femoral vein puncture during intravenous infusion and blood collection, and avoid prolonged lower limb venous infusion[1]. | October 22, Doppler ultrasound of lower limbs: blood flow in both lower limbs is unobstructed. |
