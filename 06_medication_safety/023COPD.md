### Introduction

Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD): One of the most common diseases with high morbidity and mortality rates worldwide, most prevalent in middle-aged and elderly individuals, especially the elderly [1]
A disease characterized by persistent respiratory symptoms and airflow limitation, which is preventable and treatable [2]

<!-- Slide number: 4 -->
### Introduction

COPD ranks fourth among the causes of global mortality [3]
The Global Burden of Disease study implemented in 2004 showed that approximately 3 million people die from COPD each year.
In 2011, the WHO estimated that there were 210 million COPD patients worldwide, with a total prevalence of 8.2% among people aged 40 and over in China [4]

Currently, the most important factor [5]

### Introduction

The 2011 revised edition of the "COPD Diagnosis, Management, and Prevention Strategies" added "COPD and Comorbidities," focusing on the mutual impact of comorbidities and COPD and the principles of treatment. AECOPD not only reduces the quality of life of patients but also accelerates the decline in lung function, increasing mortality rates, especially increasing mortality rates related to hospitalization, and increasing the economic burden [6]

# Nursing Measures—Guidance on Respiratory Function Exercises
Pinched-lip breathing
Breathe slightly slower than usual, must prevent dizziness and numbness in the hands due to overventilation. Exercise twice a day, 30 min/time, or 4 times/d, 15min/time, depending on individual physical condition differences.[7]

# Nursing Measures—Maintain Airway Patency
Guide deep breathing and effective coughing
Purpose: Helps to remove secretions from the distal airways,
         maintain airway patency.

<!-- Slide number: 22 -->
# Nursing Measures -- Medication Nursing
Humidification and Nebulization Therapy
Purpose: To humidify the airways and dilute sputum. Medications are added to the humidification solution for inhalation in a nebulized manner.
Method: Nebulized inhalation, q12h/each time, for 15-20 minutes each time.

# Post-discharge Management Plan
1. Indicator Testing Plan

Pulmonary function testing, 1 time/year
To understand the condition of the patient's pulmonary function decline

Exhaled nitric oxide testing, 1 time/year
To understand whether there is airway allergic inflammation

Chest X-ray,
1 time/year
