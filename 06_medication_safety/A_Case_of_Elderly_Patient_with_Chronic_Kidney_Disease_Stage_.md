## One. Introduction
Chronic Kidney Disease Staging:
In recent years, according to the internationally recognized K/DOQI guidelines, clinical staging of chronic kidney disease is based on the level of glomerular filtration rate (GFR). Chronic kidney disease is divided into 5 stages, of which stages 2 to 5 represent different stages of chronic renal failure:
1st stage: Renal damage with normal or elevated GFR [≥90ml/(min•1.73㎡)].
2nd stage: Renal damage with mild decrease in GFR [60-90ml/(min•1.73㎡)].
3rd stage: Moderate decrease in GFR [30-59ml/(min•1.73㎡)].
4th stage: Severe decrease in GFR [15-29ml/(min•1.73㎡)].
5th stage: Renal failure [GFR <15ml/(min•1.73㎡)].
【3】. You Liming, "Internal Medicine Nursing," People's Medical Publishing House, 5th Edition

## One. Introduction
Acute Heart Failure:
It refers to a clinical syndrome caused by acute onset or exacerbation of left ventricular dysfunction, characterized by marked reduction in myocardial contractility, increased cardiac load, sudden decrease in cardiac output, sudden increase in pulmonary circulation pressure, and increased peripheral vascular resistance, leading to pulmonary congestion, acute pulmonary edema, and associated with hypoperfusion of organs and tissues, resulting in cardiogenic shock.

【1】. Peng Gangyi, "Emergency and Critical Care Nursing," People's Military Medical Press, 2001.4

## Two. Case Introduction
10 years ago, due to bilateral lower limb edema, he was admitted to our hospital's nephrology department for renal biopsy, showing renal amyloidosis, and was treated with hormones and Secobarbital.
Five months ago, he had recurrent edema and was admitted to several hospitals for treatment. Edema recurred repeatedly.
Three months ago, due to anorexia and dyspnea, he was admitted to our hospital's nephrology department for diagnosis of "renal amyloidosis, chronic kidney disease stage 5" and was treated with regular hemodialysis treatment, twice a week.
On September 23, the patient had a recurrence of edema, accompanied by dyspnea and difficulty breathing, and was admitted to our emergency department. Considered acute left heart failure due to chronic renal insufficiency, he was treated with tracheal intubation and mechanical ventilation, and bedside CRRT treatment was given.
Past Medical History
  Type 2 diabetes
