Definition of Acute Cerebral Infarction
Definition: Cerebral infarction, also known as ischemic stroke, is caused by circulatory disorders in the brain, leading to ischemia and hypoxia, resulting in ischemic necrosis or softening of limited brain tissue. Acute cerebral infarction is a common acute cerebrovascular disease in middle-aged and elderly people, with more than 2 million new cases annually, of which 75%-80% will have varying degrees of sequelae.

### Notes:

<!-- Slide number: 5 -->
Definition of Rehabilitation Nursing
    Rehabilitation nursing is an important part of rehabilitation medicine, with its main objects being individuals with functional disabilities due to injury, acute and chronic diseases, and senile diseases, as well as individuals with congenital developmental disabilities. By guiding and assisting patients in rehabilitation training, and helping families understand the needs of patients, it assists patients in achieving independent self-care, so as to restore and rebuild their functions, improve their quality of life, and create conditions for their return to society. The approach of rehabilitation nursing is to transform "substitutional care" into "self-care".

Past Medical History

On November 4, 2017, the patient suddenly experienced weakness in the right limb, fell, and sustained a right intertrochanteric fracture.

Admitted to the Department of Neurology on November 4, transferred to the Orthopedic Department on November 9, underwent right intertrochanteric osteotomy and internal fixation on November 13, and discharged on November 30.

Supine position: Maintain good functional position of the right affected limb, with the shoulder externally rotated, preventing internal rotation, flex the elbow, and place a small pillow under the shoulder.
