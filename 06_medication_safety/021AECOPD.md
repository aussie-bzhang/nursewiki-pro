Pulmonary Rehabilitation

Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD) is currently the fourth leading cause of death in the world, with a risk of illness 2 times higher than heart disease and 7 times higher than cancer. In China, there are more than 40 million COPD patients, with an average of 2.5 people dying from COPD every minute.

The GOLD 2017 guidelines indicate that patients with COPD who are hospitalized due to acute exacerbation have poor long-term prognosis, with a mortality rate of about 50% within 5 years.

The GOLD 2017 guidelines also indicate that pulmonary rehabilitation can reduce the risk of re-admission and mortality in COPD patients after recent exacerbations.

Pulmonary Rehabilitation Intervention

ACBT Active Breathing Circulation Technique Training
ACBT Training Guidance
Step 1: Breathing control—diaphragmatic breathing, patient relaxes shoulders, neck, and arms, abdominal muscles are relaxed or knees are屈起 to relax abdominal muscles, one hand is placed on the sternum to limit chest movement, one hand is placed on the navel to feel abdominal起伏. Inhale through the nose, chest does not move during inhalation, abdomen swells, hold the breath for 1-2 seconds after inhalation, then exhale slowly, abdomen sinks in, and exhale as much air as possible.

Step 2: Thoracic Expansion Breathing - Perform 3-5 deep breaths, active deep inhalation, and passive relaxation exhalation. Place one hand on the chest, feeling the chest expand during inhalation. Inhale through the nose and hold the breath slightly, then exhale slowly through the mouth.

Step 3: Forceful Exhalation Technique - Composed of 1-2 huffing actions, through the opening of the glottis, starting from moderate lung capacity and continuing to low lung capacity huffing, followed by coughing or effective coughing, combined with diaphragmatic breathing, and then restarting.
