# Preoperative Preparation: Bowel Preparation
Traditional surgical requirements:
12-hour fasting before surgery, 4-hour fasting before drinking
As necessary: non-fiber liquid diet, antibiotics, and clean enema
Insertion of nasogastric tube before surgery
Enhanced Recovery After Surgery (ERAS) requirements
2 hours before surgery: 5% glucose water
Reduce preoperative thirst, hunger, and irritability;
Promote insulin secretion
Reduce insulin resistance
No insertion of nasogastric tube before surgery

# Targeted Fluid Therapy
Fluid resuscitation target: Calculate fluid volume based on cardiac output
Fluid composition: Focus on colloidal fluids
Crystalloids: Poor expansion, reduce plasma colloid osmotic pressure, may increase platelet microbubble formation, even disseminated intravascular coagulation
Colloids: Strong expansion, long retention time, can improve blood pressure, blood flow velocity, and tissue perfusion

# Early Ambulation

Extended bed rest can lead to:
