Definition and Classification of Pemphigus
Pemphigus: A group of skin and mucosal diseases occurring in the skin and mucosa with bullae as the basic skin lesion, with autoimmune and hereditary characteristics, and positive Nikolsky sign (in the epidermis).

Pemphigus

Bullous pemphigoid (bullous pemphigoid)
Until 1953, Lever believed that bullous pemphigoid was a disease different from pemphigus, a subepidermal bullous skin disease, common in the elderly, with tension bullae located on normal or inflamed skin (positive Nikolsky sign).

Bullous pemphigoid

Nikolsky sign (NIKOlsky)
By gently pushing the normal skin and mucosa with fingers, bullae can be rapidly formed, or the existing bullae can be expanded on the skin. This phenomenon is called a positive Nikolsky sign.
