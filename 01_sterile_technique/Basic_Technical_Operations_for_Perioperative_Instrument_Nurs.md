# Donning the Operating Gown
After handwashing, lift the collar of the gown and gently shake it open. While the gown is facing upwards, insert both hands and forearms into the sleeves and extend forward.

# Donning the Operating Gown
The circulating nurse assists in pulling the gown and tying the strings, then slightly pulls the bottom of the gown flat. Gently push the wearer's back and waist to indicate the completion of dressing.

# Disinfection Principles
After the iodine tincture dries, it can be removed; otherwise, it will affect the bactericidal effect.
The disinfection sequence is centered on the surgical incision, from inside to outside, from top to bottom; if it is an infected wound or anal area, it should be from outside to inside.
Surgical sponges that have already touched the edge must not return to the center for application.
The disinfection range is 20 cm outward from the incision center.

# Disinfection Range for Head Surgery
Head and forehead.

From the upper lip to the nipple, down to the anterior border of the scapula on both sides.

# Disinfection Range for Thoracic Vertebra Surgery

# Disinfection Range for Lumbar Vertebra Surgery

<!-- Slide number: 30 -->
# Disinfection Range for Kidney Surgery
From both sides over the axillary midline, up to the axilla, down to the inguinal region.

<!-- Slide number: 31 -->
# Perineal Disinfection Area
Pubic symphysis, perianal area, buttocks, and the upper 1/3 inner side of the thigh.

<!-- Slide number: 32 -->
# Hip Surgery Disinfection Area
Disinfection extends beyond the midline anteriorly and posteriorly, up to the xiphoid process, and down to the knee joint, with surrounding disinfection.

<!-- Slide number: 33 -->
# Abdominal Surgery Disinfection Area
A Upper Abdomen: Up to the nipples, down to the pubic symphysis, and laterally to the axillary midline.
B Lower Abdomen: Up to the xiphoid process, down to the upper 1/3 of the thigh, and laterally to the axillary midline.

<!-- Slide number: 34 -->
# Limb Surgery Disinfection Area
Surrounding disinfection, extending above and below each joint.
