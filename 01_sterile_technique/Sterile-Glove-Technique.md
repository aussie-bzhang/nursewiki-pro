# Purpose of Donning Sterile Gloves
To prevent the transmission of microorganisms from the patient to the medical staff
To prevent the transmission of the medical staff's own flora to the patient and reduce the opportunity for transmission between patients
To ensure sterile conditions during medical and nursing procedures

<!-- Slide number: 4 -->
# Preparation of Materials
1. Staff should be dressed neatly, wash hands, wear masks, and trim nails.
2. Prepare all materials: hand sanitizer, single-use sterile gloves, household waste bin, medical waste bin.
3. Check if the packaging of the single-use sterile gloves is intact, whether there is moisture, damage, and expiration date, and the glove size.
4. Arrange materials in an orderly manner, in accordance with sterile operation requirements.

# Seven-Step Handwashing Technique
1. First step: Wash the palm. Wet hands with water, apply hand sanitizer (or soap), palm to palm, fingers together, rub back and forth.
2. Second step: Wash the back of the fingers. Palm to back of hand along the finger缝, rub back and forth with both hands, then switch hands.
3. Third step: Wash the palm side of the fingers. Palm to palm, cross hands and rub along the finger缝.
4. Fourth step: Wash the back of the fingers. Bend each finger joint, half-closed fist, place the back of the fingers on the palm of the other hand and rotate to rub, switch hands.
5. Fifth step: Wash the thumb. Hold one hand's thumb with the other hand and rotate to rub, switch hands.
6. Sixth step: Wash the fingertips. Bend each finger joint, bring the fingertips together and rotate to rub in the palm of the other hand, switch hands.
7. Seventh step: Wash the wrist and arm. Rub the wrist and arm, switch hands.

# Steps of Operation
1. Wash hands, wear masks, hats, and trim nails.
2. Prepare a clean and dry treatment table.
3. Check the number on the outside of the sterile glove bag.
4. Check if the outer packaging of the sterile gloves is moist, damaged, and within the expiration date.
5. Tear open the outer packaging of the sterile gloves along the opening direction, unfold the inner layer.
6. Take out two pairs of gloves by pinching the folded parts of both gloves with both hands.
7. Align the five fingers of both gloves, first put on one hand.

<!-- Slide number: 9 -->
8. Use the fingers of the hand already wearing sterile gloves to insert into the inside of the other glove's folded part, and put on the glove in the same way.
9. Cross the hands and adjust the position of the gloves, place the cuff of the gloves over the sleeve of the work clothes.
10. Remove gloves: pinch the wrist of the other glove from the outside and pull it off, then insert the hand that has removed the glove into the inside of the other glove to pull it off from the outside.
11. Place the used gloves in the medical waste bin.
12. Wash hands, remove the mask.

# Precautions
Strictly differentiate between the sterile surface and non-sterile surface; hands not wearing gloves may not touch the outside of the gloves, and hands wearing gloves may not touch the inside or hands not wearing gloves.
If the gloves are found to be torn, they should be changed immediately.
When removing gloves, do not pull the edges or fingers of the gloves with force; they should be flipped off.
After wearing gloves, the arms may not hang down; they should be kept within the range of waist and shoulder movement.
