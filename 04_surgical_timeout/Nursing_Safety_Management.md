Safety and Risk

      Nursing safety is the foundation and guarantee of medical safety, the core content and eternal theme of hospital management. Paying attention to nursing safety and taking effective preventive measures can reduce harm to patients caused by nursing safety issues. Nursing safety is of great significance in maintaining the reputation of the hospital.

      Safety and risk complement each other. Managing and controlling risks well ensures safety!

# Nursing Safety Management

Definition: It refers to the scientific, timely, and effective control of various unsafe factors to ensure the physical and mental health of patients.

Purpose: Patient safety, nurse safety, hospital safety

Focus: Scientific, timely, and effective risk control

<!-- Slide number: 7 -->
# Nursing Risk Management

Definition: It involves organized and systematic activities such as identifying, assessing, evaluating, and managing nursing risks.

Purpose: To prevent risks in advance, reduce and eliminate nursing risks, minimize various losses to patients and hospitals, and achieve maximum security with minimum input. Focus: Transforming the negative handling of nursing safety incidents after they occur into positive prevention before they occur.

# Perioperative Nursing Risk Analysis

Marginal time for surgical patients

     Refers to four time periods: before the patient enters the operating room (transfer period), during the waiting process in the operating room (安置等待时段), during the recovery period (复苏时段), and during the transfer back to the ward or ICU (转运时段).

       Main risks: transfer safety, verification, changes in condition, psychological care, and more.

Risk Avoidance in Nursing Work

      Establish a risk awareness, be familiar with risk management procedures, learn to identify risk analysis and assessment, and improve one's ability to resist risks.

      Control nursing unsafe factors before the implementation of nursing measures, before each nursing operation, before the next nursing activity, and promptly eliminate them in the current nursing activity.

Safety Management Approach

Operation standardization: Prevent medical risks caused by non-standard medical operations

Careful verification: Prevent medical risks caused by human error

Thorough visitation: Carefully understand the patient's physiological and psychological condition to help the patient recover smoothly.

<!-- Slide number: 15 -->
Preoperative Management

1. Master the patient's condition, physiological tolerance, psychological tolerance, and other special issues.

2. Master preoperative abnormal vital signs, normal and abnormal test results.

3. Strengthen communication with patients, alleviate preoperative fear, and make patients actively respond to surgery. Inform patients of precautions.

4. Preoperative surgical equipment preparation, sterile operation, patient handover and verification of "bracelet" identification.

<!-- Slide number: 16 -->
Postoperative Management

1. Patients have experienced anesthesia, surgical trauma, blood loss, and fluid loss, and most have not yet escaped danger.

2. Abnormal vital signs, respiratory tract, drainage tubes, drainage fluid, and wound conditions are common, changing quickly and unpredictably.

3. Prevention of complications.

4. Risks associated with special dietary, activity, and functional training requirements.

5. Timely delivery of pathological specimens for examination.

<!-- Slide number: 17 -->
Key Points of Postoperative Management

Preventing cross-infection

Preventing wound infection

Preventing accidents during the patient's return to the ward

<!-- Slide number: 18 -->
Purpose of Perioperative Safety Management

            Surgery is an important treatment method for surgical diseases, but surgery and anesthesia are both traumatic. Good preoperative preparation enables patients to have sufficient psychological preparation and good physical conditions to safely tolerate surgery. After surgery, comprehensive treatment measures are taken to prevent and treat possible complications, restore physiological function as soon as possible, and promote the early recovery of patients, achieving patient safety and the safety of healthcare professionals.
