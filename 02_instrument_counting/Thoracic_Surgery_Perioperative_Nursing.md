## 5. Respiratory Care
Causes of Respiratory System Complications:

Stimulation from anesthesia intubation, effects of anesthetic drugs, causing cilium movement
   Decreased function, increased respiratory tract secretions;
  Long-term compression and traction of lung tissue during surgery;
  Incision pain limiting the range of respiration and coughing;
  Postoperative esophageal cancer with thoracic stomach restricting lung tissue expansion;
  Postoperative lung anastomotic edema causing difficulty in secretion excretion;
  Postoperative lung anastomotic hemorrhage causing increased viscosity of secretions.
