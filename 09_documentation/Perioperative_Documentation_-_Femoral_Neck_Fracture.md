### Knowledge

Fracture of the femoral neck below the femoral head to the base of the femoral neck

Concept
Common, accounting for about 3.58%
Aged, especially in women (osteoporosis)

Characteristics
Concept
Note

Aged patients: mostly caused by indirect violence - low-energy injury.
Young and middle-aged adults: caused by direct violence - high-energy injury.

### Notes:

### Knowledge

Bone

### Classification of femoral neck fractures
The fracture site is divided into: subcapital, transcapital, and basilar
The patient in this case belongs to subcapital fracture, the closer the fracture line is to the femoral head, the poorer the blood supply of the proximal fracture, and the greater the possibility of avascular necrosis of the femoral head.
