## Background

Problem Based Learning (PBL) is a teaching method that focuses on problem-solving, abbreviated as PBL teaching method.

Guided by teachers, centered on students, and driven by clinical problems, it guides students to learn autonomously.

1969

Professor Barrows, a neurology professor in the United States

## Objective

Improve the quality of training for perioperative nursing interns;
Study the influence of PBL teaching method on the following aspects:
Improve the understanding and memory of knowledge,
Stimulate the enthusiasm of intern nurses for learning,
Make significant progress in work ability and professional technical level in a short period of time.

## Methods

1. General Information (Training Subjects)

Experimental Group: 10 intern nurses from our hospital's operating room from 2015-2016, using PBL teaching method
                   Male 2, Female 8, Age 25-33, Education: Bachelor's 1, Associate's 9, Training Time: 3 months

Control Group: 10 intern nurses from our hospital's operating room from 2013-2014, using traditional LBL teaching
                   Male 1, Female 9, Age 24-37, Education: Bachelor's 1, Associate's 9, Training Time: 3 months

P>0.05, comparable
