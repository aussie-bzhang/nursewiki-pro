Nursing Measures to Reduce Cerebral Edema

New Concepts
  1. Reduce intracranial pressure due to changes in body position
   - The first翻身 time can be extended to 12 hours. Subsequently, turn every 2-3 hours, emphasizing double-person collaboration and axis turning.
  2. Strengthen blood pressure management
   - The rate of blood pressure reduction should not be too rapid, maintaining blood pressure at 140-160/90-100mmHg
  3. Use pain-reducing measures for invasive procedures
   - Routine application of 2% lidocaine to the nasal and pharyngeal mucosa before inserting a gastric tube to reduce sneezing, coughing, nausea, etc. induced by tube insertion, preventing the induction of cerebral hernia.
  4. Elevate the head of the bed, and continuously use cerebral cooling

Dysphagia

Concept:
      Dysphagia refers to the inability of food to pass from the mouth into the stomach due to various causes, characterized by difficulties in the process of liquid or solid food entering the mouth, swallowing, or coughing and choking during swallowing.

Caution: Risk of electrolyte imbalance, cardiac sudden death

<!-- Slide number: 17 -->
Methods for Evaluating Swallowing Function

(1) Video Fluoroscopic Cervical Myelography (VFC)
      Currently the most credible method for evaluating swallowing function. Adjust different viscosities of contrast agents, have the patient swallow, and record the entire swallowing process under a fluorescent screen to evaluate the degree and location of swallowing disorders.

(2) Swallowing Saliva Test
       The patient sits, the nurse places a finger on the patient's laryngeal and hyoid bone, and observes the number of swallowing and activity within 30 seconds.

<!-- Slide number: 18 -->
Methods for Evaluating Swallowing Function

(3) Wada Drinking Test
      The patient sits, drinks 30ml of water, and observes the process of drinking and records the time.

(4) Assessment of Feeding-Swallowing Process
       Observe feeding conditions, lip and tongue movements, mastication, transport of food bolus, and whether there is food aspiration and residue after swallowing.
