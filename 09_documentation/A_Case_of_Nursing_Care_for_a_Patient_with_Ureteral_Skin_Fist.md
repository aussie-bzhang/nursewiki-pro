# A Case of Nursing Care for a Patient with Ureteral Skin Fistula Complicated by Irritant Dermatitis and Granuloma

# Introduction
  Bladder cancer is one of the more common tumors in the body, ranking eighth in incidence, and is the most common malignant tumor in the urinary system [1].
   The treatment for infiltrative bladder cancer is radical cystectomy with urinary diversion. Some cases require an abdominal wall stoma, with urine drained through a catheter or a urinary bag [2].

［1］Wan Desen, Zhu Jianhua, Zhou Zhiwei, Pan Zhizhong. Practice and Theory of Stoma Rehabilitation Treatment [M]. Beijing: China Medical Science and Technology Press, 2006.09: 202.
［2］Hu Ailing, Zheng Meichun, Li Weijuan. Modern Clinical Nursing Practice of Wounds and Intestinal Stomas [M]. Beijing: Peking Union Medical College Press, 2010.10: 263.

# Clinical Data
   Mr. Liu, male, 74 years old, was admitted to a provincial hospital in August 2014 due to "hematuria accompanied by lumbar pain for three months and worsening for one week". On August 21, he underwent a general anesthesia for "total cystectomy with bilateral ureteral skin fistula". He recovered well and was discharged from the hospital on September 1.

  The patient came to the stoma clinic of the provincial tumor hospital on October 22, 2015, due to discomfort with the stoma.

# Causes of Irritant Dermatitis
Lack of stoma care knowledge: ① did not select appropriate stoma products; ② did not change the stoma base in time; ③ did not master the sticking technique of the stoma base
Continuous or repeated urine stimulation
Insufficient family support system

<!-- Slide number: 7 -->
# Causes of Granuloma

Foreign bodies and external mechanical stimulation cause mucosal injury, accompanied by inflammatory changes, tissue cell proliferation, and the formation of granuloma [3].
Sutures not removed in time, and the stoma base is cut too small or uneven, stimulating fibrous tissue proliferation [4].

[3] Jin Xianyin, He Jun. Clinical Analysis of 3 Cases of Granuloma Complicated by Tracheal Intubation [J]. China Clinical Research, 2011, 24(4): 287
[4] Wu Yiqin, Jin Leilei, Lu Shushu, et al. A Case Report on the Treatment of Granuloma Proliferation Around Stoma with Silver Nitrate Rod Pointing [J]. Nurse's Progress Journal, 2013, 28(11): 1033

# Health Education
Communicate with the patient and family in an affectionate manner
Strengthen the education of patients and families to help patients be able to independently complete stoma care
Explain stoma knowledge to patients through drawings, photos, and verbal explanations
Encourage patients to participate in stoma support groups
Increase fluid intake

# Effect Evaluation
1. Granulation tissue has decreased in size from the initial diagnosis.
2. The area of刺激性皮炎 around the stoma has decreased from 1.5×1.2cm² at the initial diagnosis to 1.1×0.7cm².
3. The patient's pain has decreased, with a NRS pain score of 2.
4. The ureteral stent is patent, and no infection has occurred.
5. Due to the patient's compliance, further visits were not possible.

<!-- Slide number: 17 -->
# Summary
This case utilized silver nitrate stick cauterization of granuloma, ostomy skin care powder, and skin protective film around the stoma. The new material is easy to operate, the patient experiences no pain or side effects, complications heal quickly, the patient feels comfortable, and the results are satisfactory.
The patient participates in social activities, better integrates into society, and improves the quality of life.
