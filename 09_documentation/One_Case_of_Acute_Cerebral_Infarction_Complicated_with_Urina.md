# Stroke – An Increasing Population

There are more than 7 million patients with cerebrovascular diseases in China, with 1.5 to 2 million new stroke patients each year. The annual incidence rate is 116-219/100,000 population, and the mortality rate is 60/100,000 to 120/100,000.

# Course of Treatment

6/12 Discharged
On November 20th, electrocardiogram monitoring and low-flow oxygen therapy were initiated
On November 22nd, the condition stabilized, and electrocardiogram monitoring was stopped
On November 26th, at 13:25, the patient became conscious, and electrocardiogram monitoring was stopped
On November 28th, at 15:30, difficulty in urination occurred, and a urinary catheter was reinserted
Why did urinary retention occur?

On November 27th, the urinary catheter was removed, and a urine routine examination was performed, and urination could be done spontaneously
On November 21st, at 18:30, difficulty in urination occurred, the bladder was distended, and an aseptic catheterization was performed, and a urinary catheter was left in place
On November 26th, at 9:30, the patient became drowsy, and a CT scan was performed, and electrocardiogram monitoring was initiated
On December 5th, the urinary catheter was removed, and urination could be done spontaneously

Discharge Plan
1. Strengthen nutrition; instruct the family to prepare a nutritionally comprehensive diet according to the patient's taste, with small and frequent meals.
2. Swallowing function exercise guidance and follow-up; encourage patients to do as much as they can, such as washing their face, eating, and brushing their teeth.
3. Wear non-slip canvas shoes when going out to prevent falls; provide a follow-up path card and regularly review various indicators.
