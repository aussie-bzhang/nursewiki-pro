# I. Preoperative Preparation
1. Adjust mental state, actively cooperate.
2. Complete relevant examinations, such as color Doppler ultrasound, CT, gastrointestinal contrast, etc.
3. Truthfully report the patient's condition and medical history to the doctor.
4. Family support.

# 1) Blood Donation
※ The main purpose of blood donation before surgery is to prevent excessive bleeding during surgery, determine blood type, and provide timely blood transfusion.

※ Family members should actively donate blood

<!-- Slide number: 6 -->

# 2) Respiratory System Preparation: Quit Smoking
Nicotine stimulation of the respiratory tract can cause increased secretions and airway obstruction, leading to acute atelectasis and pulmonary infection;
Causes peripheral circulation spasm, leading to failure of vascular anastomosis
Prevents excessive respiratory secretions that can cause breathing difficulties
Functional training
Deep breathing

    Coughing and expectoration

Abdominal breathing

# 3) Gastrointestinal Preparation: Fast from food and fluids
※ Adults should fast for 12 hours before surgery and 6 hours before drinking.

※ The night before surgery, 500 milliliters of warm soap water enema or oral administration of and爽 should be given to clean the intestines.

※ For two days before surgery, eat light, easily digestible, high-fiber foods such as leeks, celery, and cabbage.

# 4) Diet and Rest
Eat nutritious food before surgery

Combine activity and rest to reduce physical exertion

# 6) Skin Preparation
This is an important step to prevent incisional infection.
The patient should trim their nails, take a bath, and prepare the surgical area.
7) Material Preparation
Red belt, large safety pin, talcum powder, disposable paper cup, adult incontinence pad, urinal (male), commode (female), one bag of sugar and salt each, abdominal binder, toilet paper
8) Skin Test
Before the skin test, carefully inquire about the patient's "three histories"

# II. What to Do on the Day of Surgery

Fast from food and fluids
Remove dentures and metallic items from the body
If menstruation occurs, inform the doctor immediately
Perform relevant nursing procedures according to the patient's condition, such as measuring vital signs in the morning before surgery, inserting a gastric tube, and a feeding tube
Brush teeth, wash face, and change into a surgical gown
Ensure valuable items are properly stored
Enter the operating room 30 minutes before surgery

# III. How to Recover Quickly After Surgery
On the day of surgery, maintain a supine position with the head turned to one side. Full anesthesia patients can use a pillow after 6 hours. On the first postoperative day, maintain a semi-recumbent position, measure vital signs, and move the patient carefully to prevent the drain tube from falling out.
Move around on the bed to facilitate early bowel gas passage.
Nebulizer inhalation, routine five to six times a day, mainly to dilute sputum and promote expectoration
Cupping back to remove sputum
Provide oral care for the patient and instruct them to gargle with saltwater
Provide timely skin care to prevent pressure sores
Provide urinary tract care for patients with indwelling catheters

# Common Postoperative Symptoms
Pain
Fever
Nausea and vomiting
Bloating
Dry mouth
Urinary retention
Drain tube care
1. Securely fasten      2. Ensure unobstructed flow
3. Do not pour out the drain fluid at will
4. Straighten the drain tube before getting out of bed

#
What Family Members Should Do

1. Assist with preoperative preparation
2. Material support: arrange for daily life, financial support
3. Emotional support: understanding, support, encouragement
4. Wait outside the operating room after surgery
5. Assist with functional exercises after surgery: turning over, patting the back

#
       Turn and Thump Back
Turn: Every two hours, pay attention to the drainage tube.
Thump Back: Hands in an arch shape, from bottom to top, from outside to inside.
