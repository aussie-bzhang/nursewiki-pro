Definition of Day Surgery

    A planned hospital admission, surgery on the same day, and discharge within 24 hours.

### Notes:

<!-- Slide number: 9 -->
Definition of Day Surgery

In the early 20th century, James Nicoll of the United Kingdom proposed: for patients with certain indications, arrange for hospital admission, surgery, brief postoperative observation, recovery (usually a few hours), and discharge within a workday, with no overnight stay in the hospital, which is called day surgery (Day Surgery).

### Notes:

<!-- Slide number: 10 -->
Definition of Day Surgery
In 2003, the International Association for Ambulatory Surgery (Intemational Association for Ambulatory Surgery, IAAS) defined:
    Patients are admitted, operated on, and discharged within a workday, without the need for overnight or 23-hour hospitalization, but with strict postoperative observation. Some also call it home surgery on the same day.

### Notes:

<!-- Slide number: 11 -->
Day Surgery
1. Must be an inpatient surgery, not a routine outpatient surgery, such as small tumors; lymph node biopsy; circumcision; skin surgery;
2. The patient's condition is relatively simple, without complications;
3. The patient has certain knowledge or very strong compliance;
4. Assess the patient's social support, capable or able to care for or supervise the patient;
5. The physician also needs to meet certain entry qualifications;
6. Nursing education is very important, teaching

Future Development of Day Surgery in China

### Notes:

National Requirements
Li Tao, Director of the Research Center for Health Development under the National Health and Family Planning Commission, stated that day surgery, as an advanced management model, can effectively shorten the average hospitalization days of patients, improve the efficiency of medical services, and save medical costs, which is one of the effective means to solve the problems of difficult and expensive medical treatment for the people. The "Guiding Opinions on the Comprehensive Reform Pilot of Urban Public Hospitals" issued by the General Office of the State Council in May 2015 explicitly states that the state will gradually expand the scope of day surgery and other diagnostic and therapeutic technologies covered by medical insurance based on the standardization of day surgery, and day surgery has been included as a key content of medical reform at the national level.
