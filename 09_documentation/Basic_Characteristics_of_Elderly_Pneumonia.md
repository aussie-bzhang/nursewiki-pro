Nursing Diagnoses

Low-efficient respiratory pattern: Related to reduced lung compliance and decreased respiratory area due to pulmonary inflammation
Ineffective airway clearance: Related to thick, tenacious sputum retention in the airways and ineffective coughing due to patient fatigue
Nutritional disorder: Below the required amount: Related to recurrent infections and inadequate intake
Activity intolerance: Related to dyspnea, malnutrition, weakness, and fatigue
Constipation: Related to reduced activity, worsening condition, insufficient fluid and fiber intake
Anxiety: Related to threat to individual health and concern for prognosis
Risk for aspiration: Related to swallowing disorders
Risk for skin integrity impairment: Related to malnutrition and reduced activity
Risk for falls: Related to decreased ability and fatigue

Summary and Reflections

Reflection on the reasons for the progression of pneumonia during hospitalization: insufficient antibiotic levels, insufficient sputum drainage, inadequate patient nutrition, and possible occult aspiration.
Rehabilitation nursing should be progressive, encouraging patients to perform activities within their capabilities, self-care and management, and through case study, recognizing the importance of multidisciplinary collaboration to build a healthy barrier for patients.
The cultivation of clinical nursing thinking has been further systematized, deepened, and evidence-based in case management.
