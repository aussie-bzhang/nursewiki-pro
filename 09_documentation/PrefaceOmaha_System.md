# Preface——Omaha System——
One of the 12 standard languages recognized by the American Nurses Association (APN), a problem-oriented approach that builds its evidence for problem-solving, essentially a simplified nursing process, including the following three major aspects:

   Assessment Areas
Physiological, environmental, psychological and social, health behavior

Omaha
System
Intervention

Educational counseling and guidance, treatment and procedures, monitoring, case management interventions

Scoring
Cognitive, behavioral, status (scored on a 5-point scale)
Martin KS. The Omaha System: A Key to Practice, Documentation, and Information

# Omaha System Problem Scoring Scale
| Item | Meaning | Score |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cognitive: | The ability of the service recipient to remember and understand information | 1 point: Lack of cognition, 2 points: Slight cognition, 3 points: Basic cognition, 4 points: Sufficient cognition, 5 points: Full cognition |
| Behavior | The observable response, action, or behavior of the service recipient in cooperation | 1 point: Inappropriate, 2 points: Very little appropriate, 3 points: Occasionally appropriate, 4 points: Usually appropriate, 5 points: Consistently appropriate |
| Status | The condition presented by the patient related to subjective and objective defining characteristics | 1 point: Extremely severe symptoms and signs, 2 points: Severe symptoms and signs, 3 points: Moderate symptoms and signs, 4 points: Mild symptoms and signs, 5 points: No symptoms and signs |

Related to pseudobulbar palsy caused by pontine ischemic stroke | Patient does not aspirate, master swallowing training and eating skills | 1. Guide the patient and family to correctly eat related knowledge: 1) Pre-meal preparation: quiet, clean, comfortable environment, sitting posture during meals, appropriate swallowing training, conducive to safe swallowing during meals. 2) During meals: concentrate, do not talk, so as not to distract attention, allow the patient enough time to eat. 3) Food selection: choose smooth, uniform density, with appropriate viscosity, not easy to disperse sugar syrup consistency food; 4) Food intake: use flat stainless steel spoon and limit the amount per mouth, place the food in the posterior part of the tongue on the healthy side, conducive to food swallowing, clear the throat after each mouthful of food. 5) After meals: do not stimulate the pharynx immediately after eating, sit for at least 30 minutes, rinse the mouth, keep the oral cavity clean. 2.
