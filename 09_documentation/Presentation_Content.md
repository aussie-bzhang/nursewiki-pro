# I. Introduction

      End-of-life care refers to comprehensive care provided to terminally ill patients and their families, enabling patients to pass their final journey of life in peace, comfort, and dignity, while also allowing families to navigate through the grieving period smoothly [1].

[1] Yue Lin, Zhang Lei. Characteristics and Development Prospects of End-of-Life Care in China [J]. Nursing Journal, 2011, 26(2): 117-119.

# II. Case Introduction

Liu**, male, 66 years old, hospital number 4298, from Guangdong, native of Zhejiang, one child, wife alive, history of hepatitis B and C, lower back and abdominal pain for 2 years without medical consultation

Admitted to the Nephrology Department of Huaqiao Hospital at 4 a.m. on July 4, 2017 due to "right-sided lower back and abdominal pain for over a day"

    PCD tube placed on 16/7, removed on 15/8, 8/8 drowsiness, anorexia, unwilling to eat, fatigue, fecal incontinence, passage of black stools, reported as critically ill

Treated for "nephritis" in an external hospital's nephrology department for one month in June 2017 due to "right-sided lower back pain"

  CT and MR on 5/7 and 6/7
Indicating: liver cirrhosis, primary hepatocellular carcinoma, transferred to hepatobiliary surgery department on 7/7

Started formal end-of-life care intervention on 8/8, patient died on 21/8
