I. Preoperative Preparation

  1. Why is surgery not performed immediately upon admission?
     Surgery is an important means of treating surgical diseases, however, the trauma of surgery, anesthesia, and the stimulation of the disease itself can cause a series of neuroendocrine reactions, leading to disturbances in physiological function and varying degrees of psychological stress in the human body. This can weaken the body's defense capabilities and tolerance to surgery, directly affecting the prognosis of the surgery.
      Therefore, it is necessary to prepare in advance, correct the existing and potential physiological and psychological problems of the patient, improve the patient's tolerance to surgery and anesthesia, and minimize the risk of surgery.

I. Preoperative Preparation

2) Respiratory system preparation: No smoking for one week
Smoking itself is harmful to the body, and preoperative smoking strongly stimulates the trachea, causing an increase in sputum production and exacerbating cough, which affects respiratory function and ventilation.

Increased sputum production can lead to asphyxiation, and exacerbated coughing can affect wound healing

I. Preoperative Preparation

3) Cardiovascular system preparation: Pay special attention to the administration of antihypertensive medications in the morning of the surgery day
For patients with underlying diseases: control the underlying disease with medical treatment
   Hypertension: lower blood pressure
   Acute myocardial infarction: not suitable for surgery within 6 months after onset
   Heart failure: after the condition is controlled for 3-4 weeks

4) Digestive system preparation: No food or drink
① No food or drink: adults should start fasting and hydrating 12 hours before surgery
   To prevent vomiting during anesthesia or surgery that could lead to asphyxiation or aspiration pneumonia
   Prompt the patient to defecate the night before surgery, and if necessary, administer a laxative to prevent the relaxation of the anal sphincter after anesthesia, which could lead to fecal excretion and increase the risk of contamination.

II. What to Do on the Day of Surgery?

1. Upon waking, bathe and change into a clean surgical gown.
2. Abstain from food and water.
3. If menstruation occurs, inform the doctor immediately.
4. Perform nursing procedures as per the doctor's instructions based on the patient's condition.
5. Do not bring valuable items into the operating room.
6. Admit the patient to the operating room 30 minutes before the surgery.

III. How to Recover Quickly After Surgery?

1. Positioning
Flat on the back for 6-8 hours
Will spinal anesthesia cause a headache?
The incidence of post-spinal anesthesia headache is 3%-30%. It is caused by the poor blood supply to the dura mater and arachnoid, leakage of cerebrospinal fluid, decreased intracranial pressure, and dilated intracranial vessels, leading to vascular diseases.

2. Nutrition and Diet
Generally (non-gastrointestinal surgery): After the patient is fully awake from anesthesia, they can start eating according to the condition and doctor's instructions (high in protein and rich in vitamin C);
Local anesthesia: If there is no discomfort after surgery, the patient can start eating;
Abdominal surgery (gastrointestinal surgery): Patients need to fast for 1-3 days until the anal canal排气, and then start with a liquid diet as per the doctor's instructions.

3. Rest and Activity
Can't move after surgery? Should one rest?
Maintain a quiet room, reduce interference with the patient, and ensure they have a quiet rest;
Encourage early bed activities after the condition is stable
Bed activities: deep breathing, effective coughing, active limb movement, turning over, patting the back.
Increase lung capacity
Improve blood circulation
Prevent deep vein thrombosis
Promote peristalsis
Reduce urinary retention

Methods to alleviate pain:

        Wound physiotherapy
