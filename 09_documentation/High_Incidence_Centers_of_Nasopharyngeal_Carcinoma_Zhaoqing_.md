# Epidemiology of Nasopharyngeal Carcinoma
The occurrence of nasopharyngeal carcinoma: genetic and environmental carcinogenic factors interact and influence each other.

Three major factors lead to an increased incidence of nasopharyngeal carcinoma:
- Familial inheritance
- Environmental factors (canned food)
- Epstein-Barr Virus (EBV)

Males are more prevalent than females at a ratio of 2.4-2.8:1.

Age ranges from 3 to 86 years, with the most common age group being 30-60 years.

# Treatment Protocol
First session:
Introduce the principles of swallowing training, discuss the required skills with the patient, and address any doubts from the patient and family members; initially, train the following skills by having the patient swallow saliva.

1. Try to close the mouth as much as possible, with the lips slightly open.
2. Taste the food or drink without moving it inside the mouth.
3. When ready to swallow, do so quickly and with force.
4. Swallow the food in the mouth in one go.

Coughing may occur but try to control it; it is fine if you cannot control it.
Once the swallowing action is complete, clear your throat immediately.

# Progressive Mandibular Stretching Technique
1. First, massage the neck and throat muscles to find areas of tension.
a. Use fascial release techniques to relax the neck muscles,
b. and use throat manipulation techniques to relax the throat muscles.

2. Touch the patient's cheeks
a. Compare the muscle tension in the temporomandibular joint areas on both sides,
b. and start massaging and extending from the tighter side.

# Overcoming Nasalization Training
Nasalization is caused by insufficient movement of the soft palate, inadequate closure of the pharynx, and the pronunciation of non-nasal sounds as nasal sounds. Strengthen the strength of the soft palate muscles.

Push with palms facing each other, or push both palms downward at the same time while emitting the "a" sound. With the sudden contraction of a group of muscles, other muscles also tend to contract, thereby increasing the function of the palatal muscles;
Train to produce tongue posterior sounds such as ga, gei, ka,
Guide air flow through the oral cavity to reduce nasal leakage; such as blowing through a straw, blowing a ping pong ball, blowing a whistle, blowing a horn, blowing a whistle, playing musical instruments, blowing candles, blowing balloons, blowing feathers, blowing paper, etc., can all be used to concentrate and guide air flow

# Nasal Irrigation

Mix 1 packet of Xingbi Nasal Irrigation with 300ml of warm water to dissolve completely; irrigate twice a day, morning and evening, with water temperature controlled between 37℃ and 39℃.
**Steps:**
a. Tilt the head forward, facing the sink. When irrigating the left nostril, turn the head to the right;

Insert the nozzle into the left nostril, allowing water to enter through the left nostril and exit through the right nostril. Repeat the irrigation;
Follow the same steps to irrigate the right nostril;
Tilt the head back to irrigate the posterior nostrils. Water will exit through the mouth. Do not swallow; let it flow naturally.

If the nose is blocked, leave a gap between the nozzle and the nostril to allow water to flow out.
