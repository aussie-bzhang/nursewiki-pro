Introduction

“Cerebral stroke” refers to cerebrovascular disease caused by acute rupture or occlusion of cerebral blood vessels, leading to local or global neurological dysfunction. It is also known as “stroke,” “acute cerebrovascular disease,” or “acute cerebrovascular accident.”

Types of Cerebral Stroke

*   Cerebral thrombosis
*   Cardiac etiology
*   Cerebral hemorrhage
*   Cerebral infarction
*   Non-cardiac etiology
*   Lacunar infarction
*   Subarachnoid hemorrhage
*   Transient ischemic attack (TIA)

Nursing Experience

1. Stroke rehabilitation is a long-term battle; it is crucial to provide patients and their families with the correct understanding and emphasize the importance of rehabilitation.

2. Elderly patients with cerebrovascular diseases often suffer from symptoms such as paralysis and aphasia during the recovery period. Early rehabilitation treatment and exercise should be initiated, with planned, timed, and quantitative strengthening of passive and active movements of the paralyzed limbs. Appropriate rehabilitation plans should be chosen to minimize the occurrence of disuse syndrome.

3. Elderly patients with multiple diseases have a slow recovery, especially in the case of stroke limb rehabilitation, which requires a long process. Continuous encouragement of patients and enhancement of confidence are essential. Verbal stimulation is very important during the rehabilitation process.

4. Establishing a good relationship with patients and their families and maintaining effective communication will result in a higher degree of cooperation from both parties.

5. Personal qualifications are limited, and many considerations are not comprehensive.
