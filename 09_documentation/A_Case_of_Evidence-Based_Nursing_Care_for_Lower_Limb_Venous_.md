# Search Results
Does the use of silver ion dressings control Staphylococcus aureus infection?
Two systematic reviews (SR) and one randomized controlled trial (RCT) studies indicate that the use of silver ion dressings can effectively control Gram-positive bacterial infections and accelerate the healing of non-healing lower limb venous ulcers.

Is compression therapy provided? Is it applied using a pressure bandage or a pressure stocking?
Three SRs and one RCT study suggest that the use of compression therapy is one of the important measures for treating lower limb venous ulcer wounds. There is no statistically significant difference in efficacy between pressure bandages and pressure stockings. The choice of pressure stockings is easier to change the treatment plan during the treatment period, but the operation of pressure stockings is simpler.

# Nursing Experience
Lack: Early attention was not paid to the protection of the skin around the wound
The protection of the skin around the wound is as important as wound treatment
Observation: The condition of the skin around the wound should be observed, and venous eczema should be differentiated from ordinary damage
Need for topical medication: Timely medical intervention is required when topical medication is needed
Silver ion use: The use of silver ions should be in accordance with the product requirements
Dressing selection: Rational application of different dressings at different stages of wound healing
Health education:
The importance of persistent pressure therapy

# Insights
How to effectively reduce the recurrence of lower limb venous ulcers?
Pressure therapy is an important measure to prevent the recurrence of lower limb venous ulcers, but the compliance is low
Influencing factors: Difficulty in self-application of pressure bandages, discomfort of pressure stockings
Intervention: 1. Face-to-face communication on the importance of pressure therapy, illustrated with pictures and text;
               2. Implementation of continuing care: Multi-channel communication, such as WeChat platform
               3. Early intervention: Information and consultation through WeChat or other online platforms
