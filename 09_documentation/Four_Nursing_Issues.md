I. Introduction
Approximately 30-50% of fractures occur due to falls.
30.24% of fractures are due to falls.
Falls account for 50% of hip fractures.
The mortality rate within 3 months after hip fracture is 20%.
Posterior circulation accounts for 20% of cerebral infarction.
The common cause of death is complications such as pulmonary infection due to prolonged bed rest.

Lower limb (28.77%)
Rehabilitation nursing after hip joint replacement has been proven to be effective through effective functional exercise. Elderly patients have slow postoperative rehabilitation and complex conditions. How can specialized nurses enable elderly patients to achieve rapid rehabilitation, improve the quality of life of patients, alleviate the burden on families, and reduce hospitalization days?
