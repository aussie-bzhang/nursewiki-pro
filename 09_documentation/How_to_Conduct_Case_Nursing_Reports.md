# What is Case Nursing?
Case nursing is a summary of experiences and insights in the nursing process for a specific or several typical and representative successful cases in clinical practice. It is an in-depth analysis of a case to explore the individual characteristics and common laws of diseases in medical and nursing work.

It belongs to an experiential summary

# Why Write Case Nursing Reports?

Through the case nursing system, master the relevant knowledge and skills of special diseases, and improve the ability to solve clinical problems.

# How to Write Case Nursing Reports?
How to Select Cases?
Select representative cases in this speciality
Cases with complications, multiple nursing issues, and valuable lessons to learn from
Cases that are themselves special, achieving good results through successful nursing
Special cases: rare or uncommon cases
Nursing measures that break the routine, with innovation

# How to Write Case Nursing Reports?
Comprehensively collect information
Collect case information
(Accurate and comprehensive, through interviews, observations, and filming)
Continuous attention, record observations and thinking processes

Consult professional books and literature

# Basic Format
Introduction (Concepts related to the disease, onset, significance, etc.)
Clinical Information (Case introduction, onset, changes, and outcome of the disease)
Nursing
Nursing Assessment
Nursing diagnosis/issue, nursing goals (existing, potential)
Nursing measures and effects (how to do it)
Conclusion (What was learned? What were the gains?)
References

# Points to Note
Case introduction should be appropriate, highlighting nursing and corresponding to the theme
The purpose is to allow readers to better understand the background and basis of the nursing measures introduced in the following text, and to correspond to the theme and main content
Avoid copying medical cases, such as giving very detailed descriptions of course of disease records, vital signs, laboratory results, and medication dosages, while ignoring the theme of the article and not grasping the content closely related to nursing

# Points to Note
Nursing measures
Highlight special measures (routine nursing measures can be briefly mentioned or not written)
Pay attention to details (detailed and specific, avoid "pay more attention", "often" and so on)
Emphasize "what was done?" "How was it done?" rather than "what should be done?"

"The nurse must explain the purpose and method of sputum aspiration to the patient to obtain cooperation.
If arrhythmia or a decrease in oxygen saturation is found, sputum aspiration should be stopped immediately, and pure oxygen inhalation should be given."
