03  Teaching Skills

Self-introduction and environment introduction, involve them in everything they do: they are afraid because of the unknown;
Before imparting knowledge, let the interns be clear about: responsibility, compliance with systems and regulations, self-discipline, strict aseptic technique;

<!-- Slide number: 14 -->
03  Teaching Skills

Give students more practical opportunities and guide them carefully
Pay attention to the safety, learning, employment, and life of the students
Teach according to their abilities, pay attention to communication with them

<!-- Slide number: 15 -->
03  Teaching Skills

  Pay attention to the communication style with students (language, non-verbal)
  The art of appreciation education and criticism: find their shining points more often
  Remember their names, patience, understanding, respect, affirmation, tolerance, listening
  Smile, eye contact, physical contact
  Release your professional charm and personality charm
