# Definition of Specialty Nurse
      A Specialty Nurse (SN) is a nurse who, in a specific nursing specialty area, has proficient nursing skills and knowledge, and has completed the required education courses for specialty nurses, thereby being recognized as qualified.

# Development
The concept of specialty nurses was proposed in 1900, with the American Association of perioperative nurses (AORN) and Stanford University providing standardized training courses for operating room nurses.
Specialty nurse training was introduced in Japan, Germany, Hong Kong, Taiwan, and other regions.
In 2008, the Health Bureau of China approved the "Perioperative Nurse Specialty Qualification Certification Training Program" in the Beijing area.

# Requirements and Conditions for Perioperative and Surgical Nursing Specialists
1. Hold a Chinese nurse practice certificate and have registered as a nurse practitioner;
2. Have a passion for the job, a sense of dedication, strong work responsibility, a diligent pursuit of professional knowledge, and good medical ethics;
3. Have a diploma and more than 3 years of operating room experience; proficient in operating room specialty technical skills;
4. Possess strong clinical observation, assessment, and handling abilities in this specialty, with a certain professional theoretical foundation and specialty operation technology.

<!-- Slide number: 10 -->
# Requirements and Conditions for Perioperative and Surgical Nursing Specialists
5. Healthy physical and psychological condition
6. Possess coordination and communication skills
7. Have a high degree of self-discipline, courage to face challenges, and a spirit of continuous learning and progress
