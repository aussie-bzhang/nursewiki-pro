# Structure of the Shoulder Cuff:
The shoulder cuff is a very important structure in the body's shoulder movement, consisting of the supraspinatus, infraspinatus, subscapularis, and teres minor muscles. It originates from the scapula and attaches around the humeral head, forming a sleeve-like structure at the anatomical neck of the humeral head, participating in activities such as shoulder abduction, external rotation, and elevation.

# Function of the Shoulder Cuff:
The function of the shoulder cuff is to support and stabilize the glenohumeral joint, maintain the sealed function of the shoulder joint cavity, keep synovial fluid nourishing the articular cartilage, and prevent secondary osteoarthritis.

# Clinical Manifestations of Shoulder Cuff Injury:
The incidence of shoulder cuff injury accounts for about 17%-41% of shoulder joint diseases. Patients present with shoulder joint pain and dysfunction, manifested as difficulty in shoulder abduction and elevation or pain during abduction and elevation.

For postoperative patients with varying degrees of pain, early intervention methods are adopted, that is, when pain is felt, non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs and local cold compresses with ice bags are applied for 12 hours to alleviate pain. Instruct patients to use analgesics regularly and rationally, and adjust the dosage moderately according to the development of the patient's condition. Also, attention can be diverted from the patient's pain by changing positions, deep breathing, listening to music, and other methods. The key to functional recovery is movement, and pain is the main factor hindering movement. Shoulder joint pain and the long-term immobilization of the shoulder joint are the main factors affecting the recovery of shoulder joint function.

From the first day postoperatively to 1 week: Perform activities of the hand, wrist, and elbow under the abduction brace, and muscle contractions without tension.
① Fist Clenching Exercise: Clench the hand with force for 5 seconds, then stretch the fingers with force, 5-10 times continuously, 6-8 times a day.
② Fixed External Joint Movement: With the help of the healthy hand or others, perform active or passive movements of the elbow, metacarpophalangeal, and interphalangeal joints, and the extension and flexion of the wrist, ulnar deviation, and radial deviation, 5-10 times per joint, 5-6 times a day.
③ Static Muscle Contraction: With the healthy hand stabilizing the affected shoulder, actively perform the shrugging movement of the affected side, 10 times as one set, 2-3 sets each time, 2-3 times a day. In the first 3 days postoperatively, ice packs are applied to achieve the purpose of reducing swelling, pain, and exudation. After exercise, place an ice pack near the shoulder joint, 30 minutes each time, 2-3 times a day.

## Shoulder袖损伤的围手术期护理

**术后第 2 周 ～第 3 周**。 On the basis of consolidating the functional exercises from the previous stage, exercises are performed under the fixation of an abduction brace.

① **Flexion and Extension Exercise**: Place the hands with five fingers facing each other, with the forearm resting on the chest or with the palm flat on the table surface, so that the forearm is perpendicular to the table for extension exercises. Practice flexion with the palms facing each other, for 2 minutes each time, 4 to 5 times a day.

② **Active Stabilization of the Wrist Joint Exercise**: Use the healthy hand to stabilize the upper arm brace, and the affected limb actively performs elbow, wrist, metacarpal, and interphalangeal joint flexion and extension, gradually increasing the force and number of movements. After 1 week postoperatively, local heat敷 can be applied before exercise, or a life information therapy instrument can be used to照射 for 30 minutes, twice or three times a day, to improve blood circulation, accelerate the subsidence of swelling, and reduce pain during exercise.

**术后第 4 周 ～第 5 周**。 On the basis of the previous exercises, passive anterior flexion and elevation exercises are added. Use the healthy side hand and forearm to support the affected hand and forearm, lift them over the top of the head, hold for 4-5 seconds, repeat 4 to 5 times, and keep the upper limb in the position of the abduction brace when lowering.

**术后第 6 周 ～第 8 周**。 After the abduction brace is removed, exercises to increase the internal and external rotation of the deltoid and shoulder muscles are added.

① **Passive Internal Adduction and Internal Rotation Exercise**: Touch the opposite ear with the affected hand to practice shoulder joint internal adduction; hold a 50cm, 3cm to 4cm diameter wooden stick behind the back with the affected hand and have the healthy hand pull the stick upwards from the shoulder to continuously move the affected hand for 5 to 10 seconds, for 10 to 15 minutes each time, twice a day, to practice shoulder joint internal rotation activities.

② **Passive Circular Movement**: Bend over so that the trunk is parallel to the ground, the affected upper limb is naturally relaxed and hangs down, forming a 90-degree angle with the trunk, and use the healthy hand to support the affected forearm to perform clockwise circular movements, 10 circles as one set, practice one set in the morning and one set in the afternoon.

③ **Active Internal and External Rotation Exercise**: Frequently touch the back of the head with both hands to practice the external abduction and external rotation function; have the patient place the healthy hand behind the back to support the affected hand and touch the opposite scapula to practice shoulder joint internal rotation activities, 6 to 8 times a day.

**术后第 9 周 ～第 12 周**. On the basis of the previous stage of functional exercise, strengthen the active functional exercise of the shoulder joint, and control the external abduction and anterior flexion of the shoulder joint within 90 degrees.

① **Climbing Wall Exercise**: Stand in front of the wall, place the hands with five fingers naturally at a certain height on the wall surface, then slowly move upwards until the pain cannot be tolerated, make a height mark, 5-10 minutes each time, 2 to 3 times a day, gradually increasing the height each day.

② **Active Elevation Exercise**: Lift the affected limb over the top of the head, hold for 4 to 5 seconds, and return to the original position.

③ **Resistance Band Exercise**: Tie a loose tension elastic band on the door handle and use its tension and elasticity to perform internal and external rotation exercises to increase the range of motion of the shoulder joint. 10 times as one set, one set in the morning and one set in the afternoon.

④ **Pendulum-like Movement**: Let the affected upper limb hang naturally and use the healthy hand to help the affected limb swing back and forth, 1 to 2 minutes each time, twice a day.

⑤ **Daily Living Activity Training**: such as dressing, combing hair, fastening a belt, touching the back, etc.

**术后 第 12 周 以后**. On the basis of the previous stage of functional exercise, resistance exercises are performed to strengthen the shoulder strength and increase the intensity of active and passive training in all directions.

① **Climbing Wall Ladder Exercise**: Use a human ladder about 3 meters high, climb the ladder step by step while holding the ladder with both hands, and the human ladder goes from a low slope to a steep slope, to increase shoulder strength, once or twice a day.

② **Dumbbell Exercise**: Hold a 2kg to 3kg dumbbell in the affected limb to perform shoulder joint external abduction and elevation exercises, and exercise to the rhythm of music, 8 sets as one group, once or twice a day.

③ **Rowing or Swimming Movement**: Gradually perform resistance exercises with an elastic band.

④ **Weight Hammer or Tension Gauge Exercise**: Use a weight hammer or a spring tension gauge to exercise the muscle strength of the shoulder, once in the morning and once in the afternoon.

**关节镜下行肩袖损伤修复术** is a minimally invasive surgery with minimal tissue damage. The patient can be discharged 10-15 days after the operation, but the full recovery of shoulder joint function requires about 6 months to 1 year, therefore, discharge guidance is very important.

Before discharge, the nurse formulates relevant rehabilitation training methods according to the degree of individual shoulder cuff injury, age, nature of work, and family situation. Through explanations, demonstrations, and配合 pictures, it is necessary to teach the patient in detail the methods of rehabilitation training at each stage before discharge, so that the patient is familiar with the methods. The nurse conducts targeted regular telephone follow-ups to effectively督促 the patient to strictly implement functional rehabilitation exercises and achieve good expected effects.
