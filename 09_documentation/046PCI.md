# Introduction--Background
Acute myocardial infarction (AMI) is myocardial necrosis caused by acute and persistent ischemia of the coronary arteries, which must be treated immediately to prevent death.

With the development of AMI diagnostic and treatment methods, especially the widespread application of percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI), the mortality rate of AMI patients has decreased year by year. On this basis, cardiac rehabilitation treatment has been proven to improve the cardiac function of AMI patients, enhance their quality of life, and reduce their mortality rate [1].
[1] Rauch B, Riemer T, Schwaab B, et al. Short-term comprehensive cardiac rehabilitation after AMI is associated with reduced 1-year mortality: results from the OMEGA study[J]. Eur J Prev Cardiol, 2014, 21:1060—1069.

# Introduction--Cardiac Rehabilitation
Purpose: Increase coronary blood flow
       Increase cardiac functional reserve
       Reduce postoperative restenosis
       Enhance the effectiveness of medication
Content: Stage-based rehabilitation nursing
       Nutritional and dietary guidance
       Health education and psychological care
       Patient self-management training
Significance: Shorten the average hospital stay
             Reduce the rate of re-admission
             Enhance patients' confidence in treatment
             Improve patients' quality of life
Indicators: Ankle-brachial index (ABI)
       Left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF)

# Nursing Summary and Reflection
Nursing Summary: In this case, by working together with doctors, patients, and families to develop a cardiac rehabilitation plan, guiding and helping the patient from absolute bed rest to early bed exercises, to bedside sitting eight-segment brocade rehabilitation exercises and indoor activity training, gradually increasing the patient's activity tolerance, improving the patient's rehabilitation enthusiasm, promoting the early rehabilitation of the patient's heart, and thus improving the quality of life of the patient. No complications occurred during the cardiac rehabilitation exercise period
  Gain: This case has enhanced my professional knowledge, changed my nursing thinking mode, dared to try to apply new knowledge in the nursing process, and learned to use integrated medical care and multidisciplinary communication and cooperation to develop a more comprehensive nursing plan for patients
  Reflection: Professional knowledge is still not comprehensive enough, the follow-up and evaluation of individual cases are still not detailed enough
