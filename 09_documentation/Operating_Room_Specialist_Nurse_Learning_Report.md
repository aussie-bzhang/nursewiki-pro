With the development of society and the increasingly specialized and in-depth research in medical sciences, the specialization of nursing has become the development strategy and direction of clinical nursing practice in many countries.

Specialist nurses are playing an increasingly important role in adapting to the development of medicine, meeting people's health needs, and improving the quality of specialized and disease-specific nursing care.

Development History of Domestic Specialist Nurses

In 2000, the Shao Yifu Hospital was the first in China to establish the role of advanced clinical specialist nurses, cultivating diabetes and wound/ostomy specialist nurses.

In 2001, the Chinese Nursing Association, Zhongshan Nursing College, Hong Kong University College of Nursing, and the Colostomy Treatment Therapist Association co-hosted the Colostomy Treatment Therapist School.

In the late 1980s and early 1990s, the idea of cultivating specialist nurses in the field of specialty nursing was proposed.

In 2005, the Guangdong Provincial Health Department entrusted Nanchang Medical University and Hong Kong Polytechnic University to carry out a pilot program for postgraduate courses in specialist nurses.

In 2006, Beijing, Shanghai, Suzhou, Guangzhou, and other places had carried out training and certification for specialist nurses in diabetes, intensive care, geriatrics, infection control, and other fields.

### Notes:

Definition of Specialist Nurses

Specialist nurses are nurses who have proficient nursing skills and knowledge in a specific nursing specialty field and have completed the required educational courses for specialist nurses, and have been certified as qualified.
