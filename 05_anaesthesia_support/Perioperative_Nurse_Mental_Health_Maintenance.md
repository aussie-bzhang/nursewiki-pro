# Perioperative Nurse Mental Health
Perioperative nurse burnout:
⑴ Concept of professional burnout:
Professional burnout was first proposed by American psychologist Freudenberger in 1974, used to describe the state of exhaustion experienced by people serving in helping professions (such as police, teachers, nurses) due to long working hours, excessive workload, and high work intensity.
Professional burnout (burnout) refers to the state of physical and mental fatigue and exhaustion experienced by individuals under work pressure.

# Perioperative Nurse Mental Health
Perioperative nurse burnout:
Nurses are a high-risk group for professional burnout.
Foreign research has shown that the incidence of fatigue syndrome in nurses is 1.1% while in the general population it is only 0.2%.
The proportion of nurses affected by professional burnout in Europe is about 25%.
More than half of nursing managers in the United States experience low-level work burnout, and one-third experience high-level work burnout.
56% of nurses in Japan have high professional burnout.
The reasons are due to the youth of the nursing team, lack of work experience, and poor interpersonal relationships between medical staff.
Nurse professional burnout is a global trend.

# Perioperative Nurse Mental Health

Effective Time Management
Effective time management avoids role tension and role conflict.

# Career Planning
Concept of Career Planning
Career planning (career planning) is abbreviated as career planning.
It refers to the combination of individuals and organizations, based on the determination, analysis, and summary of the subjective and objective conditions of their own career, and a comprehensive analysis and balance of their own interests, hobbies, abilities, and characteristics, combined with the characteristics of the times, according to their own career tendencies, determining the best career goals, formulating corresponding work, training, and education plans, and taking necessary actions to implement career goals in a certain time arrangement.

# Career Planning
Nurse Career Planning Steps:
⑶ Formulating Career Action Plans and Measures
Action plans decompose career goals into short-term goals, medium-term goals, and long-term goals, among which short-term goals can be divided into daily goals, weekly goals, monthly goals, and annual goals. Medium-term goals are generally 3-5 years, and long-term goals are 5-10 years.
The decomposed goals are conducive to tracking and checking, and short-term action plans can be formulated and adjusted according to environmental changes, and effective measures can be taken for specific plan goals.
Measures mainly refer to the methods selected to achieve the established goals, such as improving work efficiency, learning knowledge, mastering skills, and developing potential.
