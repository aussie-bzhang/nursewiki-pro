Peri-Operative Transfusion Trigger Score (POTTS)
| Points | Required Epinephrine Infusion Rate to Maintain Basal Cardiac Output | Required Inspired Oxygen Concentration to Maintain SpO2 ≥ 95% | Central Body Temperature | Angina Pectoris |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Not required | ≤35% | <38℃ | None |
| +1 | ≤0.05μg/kg.min | 36~50% | 38~40℃ | Occurs during exercise, physical labor, or excitement |
| +2 | ≥0.06μg/kg.min | ≥51% | >40℃ | Occurs during daily activities or at rest |
The total score of the above four items plus 6 points is the total score of POTTS. If the total score is ≥10 points, it is counted as 10 points. That is, the minimum score of POTTS is 6 points, and the maximum score is 10 points.
