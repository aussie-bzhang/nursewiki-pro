# Overview
Colostomy has become one of the most commonly performed surgical procedures in surgery, serving as a temporary or permanent treatment measure for abdominal surgical emergencies or diseases.
Improper care of an ileostomy can easily lead to a series of complications related to the ileostomy, such as perianal skin damage.

# Clinical Data
Basic Information: Zhang**, Female, 72 years old, from Yangzhou, Jiangsu Province
Past Medical History: History of cholecystitis and appendectomy
Current Medical History: Hypertension, taking Felodipine 2.5mg Bid orally, with systolic blood pressure ranging from 130-140mmHg and diastolic blood pressure ranging from 90-95mmHg. On March 3, 2015, she was admitted to the emergency department with sudden abdominal pain and underwent "right hemicolectomy + ileostomy + adhesion release surgery".

# Assessment Tools
SACS tool (Studio Alterazioni Cutanee Stomali) for skin damage research.
SACS: It has two classification criteria, using 5 classification categories to describe the severity of lesions  'L'; using 5 anatomical quadrants to describe the location of the affected area  'T'.

<!-- Slide number: 7 -->
### Classification Categories
L = Damage

L1 = Congestive Lesion  Skin intact, redness around the stoma
L2 = Ulcerative Lesion  Open skin injury, lesions not extending to subcutaneous tissue; partial dermal damage
L3 = Ulcerative Lesion  Lesions extending to subcutaneous tissue and below; full-thickness skin damage
L4 = Ulcerative Lesion  Full-thickness skin tissue necrosis
LX = Hyperplastic Lesion  Abnormal hyperplasia (granuloma, hyperplasia)

# Summary and Reflection
In the nursing care of this case of perianal lesion, many deficiencies were found in my own assessment and operation.
I felt the importance of early stomal nursing intervention and follow-up care by specialized nurses.
I learned to use the skin assessment tool.
At the same time, there are also some confusions, due to the initial assessment not being thorough, the root cause of the patient's perianal skin problems is still unclear. This made me realize the importance of ET education and stomal assessment for stomal patients.
