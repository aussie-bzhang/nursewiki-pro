# Case Introduction

    Patient He, male, 59 years old, medical record number 1624646, underwent "ureteral peritoneal stoma surgery" due to bladder cancer six months ago. He came to the stoma wound care clinic due to "ulceration around the stoma for three days". At the time of visit, he was wearing a two-piece urinary stoma bag, complaining of right lower abdominal pain, difficulty in applying the bag, and urine leakage.

# Wound Management Process (First Visit, 10-19)

1. Disinfect the wound with iodine tincture, rinse with normal saline and dry
2. Apply skincare powder to the wound and surrounding skin
3. Spray skin protective film on the wound and surrounding skin and allow to dry
4. Apply hydrocolloid dressing to the sides of the wound to protect, use non-surgical tape to reduce tension and close
5. Fill with alginate silver dressing
6. Standard hydrocolloid dressing for external fixation
7. Leak-proof paste + convex base + two-piece urinary stoma bag + belt
8. Frequency: every other day

# Health Education
1. Care for the peristomal skin: wash the surrounding skin with warm water, pay attention to any redness, erosion, or pain around the stoma skin.

2. Select appropriate ostomy products and auxiliary supplies, perform appropriate trimming and application, effectively collect urine, avoid contamination of the wound, and facilitate the healing of the wound.

3. Correct frequency of ostomy bag replacement.

4. Advise smoking cessation.
