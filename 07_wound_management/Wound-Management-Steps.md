### Notes:

The steps for negative pressure therapy include:

1. **Preparation**: Clean the wound and surrounding skin with 0.5% povidone-iodine and normal saline.
2. **Wound Debridement**: Perform conservative debridement using appropriate instruments.
3. **Application of Negative Pressure Dressing**: Apply the negative pressure dressing to the wound.
4. **Sealing**: Ensure the dressing is sealed properly around the wound edges.
5. **Monitoring**: Regularly monitor the wound for signs of infection or healing.
6. **Change**: Change the dressing every 48 hours or as indicated by the wound condition.

Intelligent vacuum pump, easy to operate, portable, and does not affect the patient's daily life

Diabetic foot ulcers are different from common foot ulcers, with a high treatment difficulty, many complications, and a high disability rate
In this case, the patient is elderly, has poor blood glucose control, poor lower limb blood supply, and has symptoms of wound infection, so the key is to remove necrotic tissue, control the symptoms of infection, and manage exudate
