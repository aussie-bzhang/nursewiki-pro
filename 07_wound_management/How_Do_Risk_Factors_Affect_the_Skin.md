Preventive Measures > Pressure Ulcers
Thorough preoperative assessment

Standardized nursing procedures

Rational positioning of surgical position (reasonable use of position pads)
Good surgical environment (control of temperature and humidity)

Strengthened intraoperative rounds

### Notes:

<!-- Slide number: 8 -->
Preventive Measures > Electrical Burns
Select high-quality, reliable negative plates, applied to areas rich in muscles and blood vessels
Secure placement of the electrocautery pen during surgery; timely removal of scabs

Regular maintenance by a designated person, use the minimum power within the normal range
Preoperative inspection of all components, checking for proper operation

Remind doctors not to form a circuit with themselves

Keep the bed unit dry

### Notes:

<!-- Slide number: 9 -->
Preventive Measures > Fluid Extravasation, Burns

Fluid extravasation: selection of veins, treatment
Burns: correct use of warming equipment
Burns: appropriate amount of disinfectant, avoid dripping
