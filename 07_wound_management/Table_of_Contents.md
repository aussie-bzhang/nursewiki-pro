### Introduction
Abdominal multiple organ injuries refer to injuries to two or more organs within the abdomen. Due to the large amount of bleeding from the injured organs, physiological dysfunction, critical condition, complex diagnosis and treatment, there are many complications and a high mortality rate [1].

Pancreatic fistula: Pancreatic juice is excreted through non-physiological pathways after the rupture of the pancreatic duct, which is called a pancreatic fistula. It is one of the severe complications of acute and chronic pancreatitis and abdominal surgery, especially after pancreatic surgery and trauma. The incidence of pancreatic fistula after pancreatic trauma can reach 40%.

### Case Introduction
#### Surgical Condition Introduction

The patient was urgently sent to the operating room for general anesthesia and laparotomy with debridement and hemostasis. Intraoperatively, a large amount of fresh red blood was found in the abdominal cavity, totaling about 3000ml. After aspiration, the abdomen was explored.
**Surgical Plan**
- Removal of blood clots and hemostasis
- Suturing and fistulation of the duodenal distal end
- Suturing of the distal end of the stomach and performing gastrojejunostomy
- Transverse section of the neck and body of the pancreas, suture and hemostasis of the pancreatic head
- Gastrojejunal anastomosis of the pancreatic body
- Transverse section of the common bile duct and performing choledochojejunostomy
- Side-to-side anastomosis of the jejunum
The operation was uneventful, with a blood loss of 3000ml during the operation. Liver left lobe contusion and laceration
Transverse section of the gastric antrum and duodenum
Transverse laceration of the pancreatic head
Hematoma around the right kidney and retroperitoneum

### Case Introduction
#### Postoperative Treatment Plan
Postoperative admission to ICU for further treatment
**Diagnosis**: 1. Closed abdominal injury, multiple organ injuries, hemorrhagic shock 2. Pulmonary contusion and laceration
**Treatment Plan**:
- Complete relevant examinations such as electrocardiogram and chest X-ray; dynamically observe changes in blood routine, amylase, etc.; adjust fluid resuscitation according to PICCO related indicators
- NPO, nasogastric decompression, fluid resuscitation, necessary retransfusion, maintain blood pressure, "Nasid" acid suppression and gastric protection, "Somatostatin" hemostasis, "Junte + Vancomycin + Diflucan" prophylactic and empirical anti-infection, maintain electrolyte and fluid balance and nutritional support, etc.
- Adjust the treatment plan in a timely manner according to the patient's condition changes and relevant test results

Improved drainage bottle
Use 3M dressing to seal the wound drainage tube and surrounding area to form a closed drainage system

Specimen bottle - collect drainage fluid
Water-seal bottle - tube inserted below the water level
Safety bottle - connected to the negative pressure suction device, water level is 8cm, when the negative pressure is too high, air will enter the safety bottle to relieve pressure and keep abdominal organs from being damaged
