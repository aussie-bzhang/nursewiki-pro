**Introduction**
Abscess: An acute suppurative infection or pilonidal cyst infection of multiple adjacent follicles and their associated sebaceous glands or sweat glands.
Abscess incision: A surgical procedure to incise the abscess to expel pus or necrotic tissue, with the aim of relieving local pain, preventing the spread of inflammation, and preventing infection from entering the bloodstream.
Simple incision and drainage with antibiotic treatment is given because the boundary of necrotic tissue is unclear, which is prone to form fistulas and persist for a long time. The method of dressing change and the choice of dressing materials have an impact on the healing of the wound, and improper handling can easily lead to chronic wounds.

**Nursing Measures**
        ------Local Wound Care

First initial visit (10.17)

1. Abscess incision and drainage under local anesthesia
2. 0.9% NS cotton balls to clean the wound, conservative
3. Debridement with sharp instruments to remove purulent secretion, necrotic tissue, etc., from the wound
4. Silver ion oil gauze is placed to fill the wound bed, for drainage and anti-infection
5. Outer layer covered with gauze pad to absorb exudate and protect the wound bed

Discussion
1. Selecting an appropriate time for incision and drainage of the abscess is a prerequisite for healing.

During the formation of the gluteal abscess, a large number of neutrophils infiltrate the tissue, leading to necrosis. The primary characteristic of the disease in the early stage is infection, while the main issue in the later stage is mainly the necrosis and clearance of the tissue.

Incision and drainage remove the necrotic tissue and cells within the abscess cavity and can stimulate the proliferation of macrophages, endothelial cells, and fibroblasts, thereby accelerating healing.

Wu Zai-de. Surgery [M]. 5th ed. Beijing: People's Medical Publishing House, 2000: 176-181.

Discussion
2. Applying the theory of moist wound healing, the correct use of dressings can effectively promote wound healing.

Nanometer silver dressing can inhibit bacterial growth, not adhere to the wound, and has the functions of drainage and moisture retention, promoting healing.

Foam silver dressing absorbs exudate, resists infection, flattens granulation tissue, and inhibits its excessive growth.

Hydrogel dressing maintains a moist environment continuously, absorbs exudate, and promotes the growth of granulation tissue and the migration of epithelium.
