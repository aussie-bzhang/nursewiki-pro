# Introduction

Skin and mucosal separation at the colostomy site is an early complication after colostomy surgery.

It commonly occurs within one week after surgery【1】，accounting for 17-27% of colostomy complications【2】.

Causes

  Difficult wound healing  Colostomy retraction  Colostomy stenosis

# Nursing Measures - Psychological Care
Stress affects cell vitality, thereby delaying wound healing【3】.
Explain the purpose and methods of treatment.
Participate in the Sunshine Home class and patient experience sharing.
Engage in ostomy care to understand the progress of wound healing and promote psychological rehabilitation.
Spouses should show concern and understanding for the patient and assist with ostomy care.

# Summary
The wet healing method is used to treat ostomy mucosal separation, preventing leakage and protecting the wound from infection.
The treatment of ostomy mucosal separation caused by infection should be based on controlling infection and ensuring adequate drainage.
New-type dressings with hydrophilic fibers and silver have long-lasting antibacterial properties, vertical absorption, painless debridement, and reduce the time for dressing changes.
There is evidence that preoperative evaluation by an ostomy therapist, designing the position of the skin ostomy, and providing education to patients can help reduce adverse outcomes【6】.
Early observation, proper management, health education. Reduce the occurrence of complications.
