# Perception
For wounds that are difficult to heal after trauma, both effective exudate management and promotion of granulation tissue growth are essential. In this case, the wound had a large amount of exudate, was deep and wide, and had an undermining defect, with slow granulation tissue growth. Additionally, the patient had factors that were不利于 wound healing, such as obesity, smoking, and excessive limb movement. During the wound care process, high-absorbent and granulation tissue-promoting dressings were used to reduce exudate, and the wound bed was prepared for taping with a non-surgical tape to minimize debridement and prevent the risk of wound re-opening, accelerate wound healing, and alleviate the patient's financial and pain burden.
