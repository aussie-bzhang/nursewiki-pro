## I. Introduction         Incidence
Vascular dementia (VaD) is one of the common causes of senile dementia.

Europe
The prevalence of VaD in the 65-69 age group is 0.3%, increasing to 5.2% in people over 90 years old.

China
Currently, about 2.4 million new cases of cerebral stroke occur each year, with 1.1 million deaths due to stroke. There are over 11 million existing stroke patients, and the incidence of cognitive impairment after stroke is as high as 80.9%, including 48.91% with non-dementia cognitive impairment and 32.05% with dementia, which imposes a serious burden on society and families.

Tian Shanshan, Xu Shaohong, Hu Ruiping, et al. The effect of cognitive function training on the improvement of swallowing function in patients with swallowing dysfunction and cognitive dysfunction after brain injury [J]. Shanghai Medicine, 2017, 38(13): 17~21

### Case Experience
1. This case involves a patient with cerebral infarction and vascular dementia. From the beginning, the patient rejected me and had very poor compliance with treatment and nursing. Through the guidance of the head nurse and the teaching teacher, and after efforts were made, the patient and the family have gained trust and cooperation. Now, the patient has good contact and actively participates in rehabilitation training.
2. During the process, I gained rich clinical experience and mastered the use of various assessment tools, thereby more accurately identifying existing or potential nursing problems, implementing corresponding nursing measures in a timely manner, improving the quality of life of patients, and establishing deep feelings with patients and their families.
3. As elderly patients have many underlying diseases and my experience needs to be improved, I will continue to learn hard and summarize experience in clinical nursing in the future, providing better services for patients, and achieving the best nursing outcomes for patients.
