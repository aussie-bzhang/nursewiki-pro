With the aging of the population, there is an increasing trend in elderly patients with acute myocardial infarction (AMI). Clinical symptoms are atypical, with relatively few patients presenting with severe chest pain. Many patients present with symptoms such as cough, dyspnea, and respiratory distress, as well as left heart failure as the initial symptoms. Heart failure is often one of the causes of death.

Definition:
It refers to the occlusion of the coronary artery, the interruption of blood flow, and the local necrosis of part of the myocardium due to severe and persistent ischemia.

A 26-year follow-up study in the United States showed:
Incidence rate of AMI (35-84) in the population: males 71‰, females 22‰
              Among the 55-64 age group: males 91‰, females 25‰
                      65-74 age group: males 119‰, females 51‰
                      75-84 age group: males 168‰, females 90‰
