Introduction

Background

Cerebral hemorrhage is a common disease in the elderly, with high rates of disability and mortality, which imposes a heavy burden on patients' families and society [1.2]. Elderly patients with cerebral hemorrhage are prone to long-term bed rest and have a high risk of developing pulmonary infections [7].

Due to swallowing difficulties or altered consciousness in patients with cerebral hemorrhage, gastric contents may be aspirated into the lungs, leading to aspiration pneumonia.
