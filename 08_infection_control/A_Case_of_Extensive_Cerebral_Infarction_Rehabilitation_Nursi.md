  Introduction
GORDON Model

    Gordon model case management refers to the use of Gordon's "functional health patterns" assessment model to explore, analyze, and summarize reports for specific cases.
    Gordon's eleven functional health patterns are a structure of nursing assessment, forming a standard form for collecting basic patient information. The assessment method using functional health patterns can directly identify patient nursing issues and clarify patient nursing needs.

<!-- Slide number: 4 -->
  Introduction
       Extensive cerebral infarction is caused by occlusion of large arteries in the brain, especially the occlusion of the middle cerebral artery leading to extensive ischemic necrosis in one hemisphere of the brain, which has a serious impact on people's daily activities [1]. More than 75% of survivors have varying degrees of sequelae, including limb, language, and cognitive impairments, severely affecting the patient's ability to perform Activities of Daily Living (ADL). 

    Therefore, the implementation of rehabilitation nursing is urgently needed and necessary, which can improve the patient's ability to perform ADL and is of great significance for helping them return to the family and society.

Summary
The Gordon Functional Health Pattern Assessment Model focuses on the overall function of the whole person for assessment, which can reflect the overall concept of a person in the dynamic life process, and each function is an expression of biology, psychology, and spirit.
