With the development and deepening of hospital information management construction, the use of advanced radio frequency identification module technology, combined with database technology, communication and information technology to solve the management and data collection of a large number of surgical instruments in operating rooms, thus changing the traditional working mode. In the past, operating rooms needed to manually record all the situations of instruments, and had to check the expiry date of sterilization packages one by one every day. The establishment of the surgical instrument traceability management system makes up for this gap, as it can accurately record all the data of surgical instrument processing, and realize the quality traceability and control in the process of surgical instrument management.

System Introduction

Using the information generated at each stage to achieve management of the entire process, ensuring a complete, flexible, and real-time instrument kit process traceability system.

By collecting information on the working status of instruments and personnel at each stage, all information of the instrument kit throughout the entire work process can be traced.

### Notes:
1. After importing the preoperative preparation form data, the system processes it according to the preoperative preparation form. The required equipment, supplies, and disposable items from the preoperative preparation form are allocated to the equipment packing area, supply packing area, and disposable material packing area for packing respectively. The system can automatically assign the tasks requiring packing from each packing department on the preoperative preparation form to their respective terminals, improving the current method of printing large amounts of paper lists.
4. Nursing staff collect surgical instrument kits from the sterile storage room of the operating room. After scanning the nursing staff card and the RFID tag on the surgical instrument kit, the system automatically matches it with the preoperative preparation form. After verification, the surgical instrument kit is issued. The information verified includes (kit recipient, pick-up time, type of surgical instrument kit, expiry date, operating table number, and session number, etc.).
After the nurse completes the preoperative preparation, the RFID tag on the surgical instrument kit is scanned. The system automatically completes the matching of the preoperative form with the operating surgeon, automatically verifies the patient ID, and ensures that the surgical form corresponds to the chief surgeon one by one, avoiding information confusion due to a mismatch between the preoperative preparation form and the chief surgeon, and excluding the possibility of medical accidents. The nurse checks the item list inside the kit through the information on the label of the surgical instrument kit (including supply kits and disposable items) to ensure that the instruments inside the surgical instrument kit are clear and accurate, providing a basis for postoperative counting.
5. The nurse counts the types and quantities of instruments inside the surgical instrument kit (including supply kits and disposable items) through the information on the label of the kit to ensure that all instruments inside the surgical instrument kit are collected in a timely manner, avoiding the phenomenon of instruments being left inside the patient, and completely excluding such medical accidents. According to the individual needs of doctors, modifications and customization of instruments in the surgical instrument kits can be made, and customized surgical instrument kits for the individual needs of a specific doctor can be created (such as adding surgical instruments, using instruments with longer handles, etc.).
2. After receiving the allocated surgical instrument kit, the nurse reads the RFID tag information using a handheld RFID reader/writer device. The system automatically lists the categories of surgical instrument kits and the corresponding detailed surgical instruments, the corresponding surgical procedure form, the operating table number, the chief surgeon, etc., in a menu format for verification. After confirmation, the system automatically records the date and information of the packing staff in the RFID tag and the handheld machine database. The data from the handheld machine is uploaded to the database server for backup at the end of the shift, providing data information for the statistics of the nurse's packing work and the formation of packing work reports.
3. After the surgical instrument kit is disinfected and removed from the disinfection pot, the disinfection nurse can log into the disinfection system module of the handheld machine with a card. The system verifies whether the disinfection in the disinfection pot is qualified based on the label on the disinfection pot. If qualified, the disinfection nurse scans the label on the instrument kit, writes the qualified information into the label, and the system automatically records the information of the disinfection verification personnel, the disinfection pot number, and the verification time, etc. After the information verification is completed, the data is uploaded to the server for the traceability of the surgical instrument kit information. After the surgical instrument kit is disinfected, it is sent to the sterile storage room of the operating room for storage.
After the surgical instrument kit is disinfected, it is stored on the designated surgical instrument shelves in the sterile storage room of the operating room, arranged separately for each department and emergency. Through the RFID reader/writer device, the system automatically records the category of the surgical instrument kit, the storage time, the retrieval time, and the expiry date, etc. Inventory and expiry date inquiries can be made through the information management query system.
