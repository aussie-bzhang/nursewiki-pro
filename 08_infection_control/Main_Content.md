# Basic Concepts of Management
DEFINITION

    The process of effectively integrating resources owned by an organization through activities such as planning, organizing, commanding, coordinating, and controlling in a specific environment to achieve organizational goals.

PURPOSE

    The purpose of management in any field is to achieve management goals "more effectively," that is, to pursue the highest efficiency and the best results.

OBJECT

    The knowledge system of management is a science with clear concepts, categories, universal principles, and principles. Its objects include: human resources, material resources, financial resources, time, information, etc.

<!-- Slide number: 15 -->
# Characteristics of Management

Artistry of Management

Manager's ability = scientific knowledge + management art + experience accumulation
    Management is a unity of science and art. Artistry refers to the innovative ability and skills of managers to flexibly handle problems on the basis of understanding objective laws. The efficiency and effectiveness of modern enterprise work largely depend on the management art of managers.

Importance of Corporate Culture

    First-class enterprises do culture, second-class enterprises do standards, third-class enterprises do brands, fourth-class enterprises do services, and fifth-class enterprises do manufacturing.
