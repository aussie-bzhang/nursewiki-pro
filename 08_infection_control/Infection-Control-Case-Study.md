**I. Introduction**

Acute myocardial infarction (AMI) is a condition caused by prolonged ischemia leading to myocardial cell death. It is characterized by acute onset, rapid progression, and high risk of prognosis [4]. If not treated promptly, it can lead to death. Domestic data shows that the incidence rate is 45/100,000-55/100,000, with an increasing incidence rate in middle-aged and young adults (＜60 years old) [2, 3]. International data indicates [1] that 66 out of every 100,000 people are affected. Studies have shown [5, 6] that effective nursing interventions can improve the quality of life for patients. Therefore, individualized nursing measures are particularly important during the treatment process.
