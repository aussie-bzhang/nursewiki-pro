# Patient's Disease Information
Diagnosis: Behçet's Disease (also known as Behçet's Syndrome, oculo-oral-genital syndrome)
Chief Complaint: Gluteal ulcer for 1 month
Brief History of Treatment: The patient reported bilateral inguinal erythema, erosion, and exudation after wearing dance pants for half a year ago. The pain was significant, and the condition worsened after self-medication. She was admitted to a hospital in November 2015 for treatment and was discharged after recovery. One month ago, due to the recurrence and aggravation of gluteal ulcers, she was readmitted to the hospital on March 22, 2018.
Past Medical History: Hypertension (grade 3)
