# Introduction
Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD) is a preventable common disease characterized by persistent airflow limitation. The airflow limitation progresses progressively, accompanied by increased chronic inflammatory response in the airways and lungs to harmful particles or gases.
China's 2007 survey: The prevalence rate of ≥40-year-old individuals is 8.2%
By 2020, COPD will become the third leading cause of death worldwide

Approximately 43 million people have COPD

# Correlation between Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease and Respiratory Function Exercise
1. Performing respiratory function exercises during the remission phase of COPD can improve the muscle strength and endurance of the respiratory muscles, reduce respiratory efficiency, alleviate respiratory muscle fatigue, increase alveolar ventilation, improve respiratory function, alleviate dyspnea, and improve the quality of life. However, due to the existence of compliance issues, it seriously affects the effect of respiratory exercise. Numerous literature reports indicate that respiratory function exercises combined with routine treatment are more beneficial for improving lung function, enhancing physical endurance, and improving the quality of life. However, due to the lack of knowledge about respiratory function exercises and poor execution of behavior among COPD patients, there is a correlation between the two, but there are also other important intermediate variables. Research results suggest that healthcare professionals should pay attention to respiratory function education for COPD patients, take targeted health education measures in various forms. At the same time, attention should be paid to the influence of intermediate variables between knowledge and behavior. By changing intermediate variables, changing patients' beliefs, improving their confidence, and maximizing the impact of knowledge on behavior. In addition, caregivers play an important role in the execution of respiratory function exercise behavior of patients. Therefore, during the implementation of respiratory function exercise interventions, not only should relevant knowledge be provided to patients, but also health education should be provided to caregivers, establishing a communication platform. Through the collaborative efforts of family members and patients, patients and patients, experts and patients, etc., patients' beliefs can be changed, their confidence improved, and their behavior improved.

Methods of Respiratory Function Exercise

Through the formation of a weak resistance by pinching the lips to prolong the exhalation time, airway pressure is increased, and airway collapse is alleviated.
Method: Close the mouth and inhale through the nose, pinch the lips and exhale slowly, while contracting the abdomen. The inhalation-exhalation ratio is 1:2 or 1:3.

# Methods of Respiratory Function Exercise

Abdominal Breathing
Patients take an upright position, weak patients can take a sitting or semi-recumbent position, place the left and right hands on the abdomen and chest respectively, inhale through the nose, try to protrude the abdomen, exhale through the mouth, while contracting the abdomen, keeping the chest minimum movement, slow deep exhalation, train 3-4 times a day, repeat 8-10 times each time

# Learning How to Cough Effectively
Controlled Cough Technique
Huffing Technique

1. Find a comfortable sitting position
2. Tilt the head slightly forward
3. Keep the legs on the ground
4. Breathe deeply through the nose

5. Then exhale briefly and not too forcefully while the mouth is open. It's like breathing onto a glass window as you normally would.
6. Repeat one or two times
Do not exhale too forcefully!

# Discharge Instructions
1. Strengthen overall exercise and respiratory function, and practice respiratory exercises to enhance resistance, ensure adequate sleep and hydration, and perform effective coughing and expectoration when phlegm is abundant.
2. Pay attention to oxygen therapy safety and regular replacement, cleaning, and disinfection of oxygen therapy devices. The oxygen flow rate is 1-2 liters/minute. Understand the purpose and necessity of oxygen therapy, and ensure a daily oxygen therapy time of more than 15 hours, especially do not interrupt during the night. Adhere to the home activity plan, pay attention to safety to prevent falls and balance, and self-monitor.
3. Guide patients to cultivate interests and hobbies, encourage participation in household chores, work, and social activities within their capabilities.
4. Pay attention to warmth, prevent colds, and ensure good indoor air circulation.
5. Provide discharge education materials, guide regular medication, and schedule regular outpatient follow-up appointments.
6. Inform the attending physician of the outpatient clinic visit schedule.

# Summary

Experiences
1. Case management has taught me many new knowledge, especially the use of specialized scales for specialized assessments.
2. Negotiating with the patient and family to develop an individualized home exercise plan is of great significance in promoting patient cooperation in treatment and nursing, and in promoting a good outcome for the patient. This has led to patient cooperation, and tracking the patient's condition for two weeks has shown that the patient is generally satisfied with their current living situation and has received support and cooperation from the patient's family.
3. By regularly following up with phone calls to督促 patients to adhere to the home exercise plan, continue medication, engage in appropriate social activities, and pursue certain hobbies, the ultimate goal of reducing hospitalization time, alleviating economic burden, and improving the quality of life has been achieved.
Shortcomings
1. Nursing assessment was not comprehensive, and the collection of information was incomplete.
2. The nursing plan was only developed for the patient, and the quantification of nursing outcomes was insufficient.
