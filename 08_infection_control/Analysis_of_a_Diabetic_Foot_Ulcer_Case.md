Assessment Methods - Appearance
Granulation tissue: Granulation tissue refers to the proliferation of small blood vessels and connective tissue that gradually fills the wound.
Healthy granulation tissue: Beefy, bright red, soft, and shiny

Decayed tissue: Loose, yellow, and lacks vitality

Necrotic tissue: Brown or black, lacks vitality

Epithelialization: Appearance of epithelial cells, pink
Infection: Red, swollen, hot, and painful around the skin

Nursing Measures

1. Follow medical advice to use an air mattress to avoid prolonged pressure on the local skin.

2. Keep the bed unit clean, change the bed linen in a timely manner, and provide the patient with timely changes of patient attire.

3. Strengthen skin care. Keep the skin clean, follow medical advice to insert a urinary catheter for the patient, and avoid exposing the skin to a moist environment caused by sweat, incontinence, and wound drainage fluid.

<!-- Slide number: 33 -->
3

Nursing Measures

4. Perform regular turning of the patient and establish a turning card for routine ward rounds.

5. Monitor the patient's body temperature strictly as per medical advice and provide the patient with a fever-reducing patch.

6. Administer penicillin intravenously as per medical advice to control infection.

<!-- Slide number: 34 -->
3

### Nursing Measures
7. Monitor the patient's blood glucose levels strictly as per medical advice, as good control of blood glucose is beneficial for wound healing.

8. Conduct pressure ulcer assessments and record them at each shift.

<!-- Slide number: 35 -->
3

### Nursing Measures

7. Provide the patient with a diabetic diet as per medical advice and strengthen dietary education for the patient's family.

The patient's albumin level has increased from 26.7g/L at admission to 33.0g/L.

### Nursing Measures

8. Conduct an effective assessment of the wound and select the appropriate material for regular dressing changes.
