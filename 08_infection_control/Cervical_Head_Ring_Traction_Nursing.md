Discharge Instruction
Discharge instruction

7

For the first month postoperatively, bed rest is the main activity, but patients can get up to eat, use the toilet, and take short walks indoors; an orthosis must be worn during activity.

From 1 to 3 months postoperatively, appropriate indoor and outdoor activities, with walking as the main activity; an orthosis must be worn during activity.

From 3 to 6 months postoperatively, increase outdoor activities, with walking as the main activity; no orthosis is needed indoors, bending over is allowed, and an orthosis must be worn outdoors.

From 6 to 12 months postoperatively, no orthosis is needed, and activities such as jogging, swimming, skipping rope, and lifting objects weighing no more than 10 catties are allowed.

From one year postoperatively, all activities can be performed except for heavy lifting and intense competitive activities (such as football, basketball, wrestling, etc.).

Postoperative follow-up X-rays should be taken at the hospital at 3, 6, 12, and 24 months.
