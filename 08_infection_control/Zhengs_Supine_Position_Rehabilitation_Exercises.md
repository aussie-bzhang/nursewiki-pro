Definition
          Dermatomyositis is an autoimmune disease characterized by skin and muscle lesions.
    Patients often have multiple systemic complications, such as heart, lung, and kidney, with interstitial pneumonia being the most common and severe.

### Notes:

<!-- Slide number: 5 -->
Background

      Dermatomyositis (DM) is a type of idiopathic inflammatory myopathy characterized by erythema, edema, muscle weakness, and inflammatory changes in the skin and muscles.

    The incidence of dermatomyositis complicated by interstitial lung disease (ILD) ranges from 5% to 64%, with a very high mortality rate. Secondary respiratory failure is the main cause of death in patients with dermatomyositis.

<!-- Slide number: 6 -->

Clinical Manifestations of Dermatomyositis Complicated with Interstitial Lung Disease

Clinical Manifestations
Mainly fever, dry cough, and hypoxemia, which may even lead to respiratory failure and death

Respiratory System
Dyspnea, respiratory distress, cough, sputum production
Swelling of lower limbs, joint swelling
Joint pain

Clinical Features

Skin System
