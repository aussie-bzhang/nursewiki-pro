## Three: Postoperative Nursing
### Post-anesthesia Nursing Routine
Follow the routine nursing after general anaesthesia or epidural anaesthesia.
General anaesthesia patients are monitored until they are conscious, supine with the head elevated for 6-8 hours, and the head turned to one side.

<!-- Slide number: 20 -->
## Three: Postoperative Nursing
### Postoperative Position
After returning to the ward, a thin pillow is placed under the lumbar and sacral area to relieve pressure on the sacral tail, prevent pressure sores, and change every 1-2 hours;
After 6-8 hours of return to the ward and stable blood pressure,翻身 can be timed;
After 12 hours of surgery and stable blood pressure, a semi-recumbent position can be adopted;

## Postoperative Nursing
### Drainage Tube Care
Properly secure all drainage tubes to maintain unobstructed flow.
Observe the color, nature, and volume of the drainage fluid, and handle any abnormalities promptly.
Instruct the patient's family on the use of the drainage bag and vacuum aspiration ball, as well as the precautions.

## Postoperative Nursing
### Postoperative Activity
Immediately instruct the family to massage the patient's lower limbs and lumbar region upon returning to the room, encourage the patient to move their lower limbs frequently, and turn over in bed to promote bowel movement, prevent venous thromboembolism, and pulmonary complications.
Encourage early ambulation as soon as the condition permits.

## Postoperative Nursing
### Postoperative Diet

After passing gas, transition from fluid to semi-liquid to regular diet, eat in small and frequent meals, gradually increase intake, and avoid gas-forming foods early on.
Consume low-fat, high-protein, high-calorie, and high-vitamin diet within one month postoperatively, and drink plenty of water.
