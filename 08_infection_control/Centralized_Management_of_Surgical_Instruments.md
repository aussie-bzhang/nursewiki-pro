# Definition of Biofilm
?
What is biofilm?

Biofilm [Biofilm] refers to microorganisms adhering to the surface of specific items, which, through the secretion of extracellular polymeric substances [EPS], form a membranous bacterial community with bacteria, commonly known as biofilm.
