<!-- Slide number: 2 -->

### Steps for Donning and Doffing Isolation Gowns

**Four. ### Steps for Donning and Doffing Isolation Gowns

**Five. Hold the collar with both hands, fasten the collar button from the center of the collar along the edge to the back, then tie the sleeve cuffs (at this point, the hands are contaminated), and loosen the belt buckle.**

### Selected Courseware PowerPoint

<!-- Slide number: 10 -->
### Steps for Donning and Doffing Isolation Gowns

**Six. Pull one side of the isolation gown about 5cm below the waist and gradually pull it forward until the edge is visible, then pinch it; do the same with the other side, taking care not to touch the inside of the gown. Then, with both hands behind, align the edges, fold them to one side, press one hand on the folded area, and use the other hand to pull the belt to the back to press the folded area, cross the belt behind, and tie it in front.**

### Key Points for Donning and Doffing Isolation Gowns

1. Prepare all necessary items before putting on the isolation gown.
2. Ensure the isolation gown is of appropriate length; if there are any holes, they should be patched promptly.
3. Keep the collar clean; when fastening the collar, the cuff should not touch the face, collar, or hat. The entire collar of the isolation gown should cover the work clothing.
4. After wearing the isolation gown, restrict movement to designated areas only and do not enter the clean zone.
5. When hanging the isolation gown, ensure that the sleeves are not exposed and that the gown edge does not cover the clean area.
6. Change the isolation gown daily; if it becomes damp or soiled, it should be replaced immediately.
