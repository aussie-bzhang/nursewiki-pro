1. Sterilization Effect Monitoring of Disinfection and Sterilization Methods Used for Sterile Items in Operating Rooms

<!-- Slide number: 5 -->
Concepts
1. Disinfection: The process of removing or killing all pathogenic microorganisms except spores, to achieve a harmless level.
2. Sterilization: The process of killing or removing all microorganisms from the external environment. This includes bacterial spores and fungal spores.

<!-- Slide number: 6 -->
Significance of Disinfection and Sterilization
- It is a basic operational standard for clinical departments
- The infection rate of surgery is closely related to disinfection and sterilization technology

2. Common Chemical Disinfectants Used in Operating Rooms

<!-- Slide number: 15 -->
Factors Affecting the Bactericidal Effect of Chemical Disinfectants:
- Concentration and action time of disinfectants.
- Temperature and relative humidity.
- Acidity and alkalinity.
- Inhibitors.
- Organic matter.
- Types and quantities of contaminated microorganisms.

<!-- Slide number: 16 -->
Types of Common Chemical Disinfectants

- Peroxides
- Chlorine-containing disinfectants
- Alcohol-based disinfectants
- Iodine-containing disinfectants

<!-- Slide number: 17 -->
Peroxides
Hydrogen peroxide (peroxide): concentration 3%
- Action: Disinfection and decontamination
- Application: Infected wounds, neurosurgical procedures, debridement surgery, etc.

<!-- Slide number: 18 -->
Precautions for Use
- Avoid light, heat, and store at room temperature
- Corrosive to metal
- Bleaching effect on fabrics
- Prevent from splashing into eyes or mucous membranes
- Rinse with clean water immediately if splashed

<!-- Slide number: 19 -->
Chlorine-containing Disinfectants
- Suitable for disinfection of items, floors, surfaces, secretions, and excreta
- Preparation method:
  - According to the effective chlorine content of the product, the required concentration and quantity
  - Add clean water + chlorine disinfectant tablet, dissolve thoroughly
  - Use a special concentration card to monitor the concentration
- Common concentrations: 250mg/L; 500mg/L; 1000mg/L, 2000mg/L
- Disinfection method: immersion method, wiping method

Use Method
1. Immersion method:
   - Bacterial propagules: use a disinfectant solution containing 500mg/L of effective chlorine, soak for >10min
   - For items contaminated with pathogens transmitted through blood, mycobacteria, and bacterial spores, use a disinfectant solution containing 2000mg/L-5000mg/L of effective chlorine, soak for >30min
2. Wiping method: the disinfection time and concentration are the same as the immersion method

<!-- Slide number: 21 -->
Precautions

Store in a cool, dark, and dry place
Use freshly prepared chlorine disinfectant solution, with a shelf life of ≤24h
Wear gloves and masks when preparing the solution
Corrosive to metal
Rinse or wipe with clean water after immersion or wiping
Cover the container with a lid when soaking
Corrosive and bleaching effect on fabrics
Monitor the concentration before each use (nurse performs)

<!-- Slide number: 23 -->
Alcohol-based Disinfectants
Ethanol (Alcohol)
1. Common Concentration: 75%
2. Uses: Skin deiodination disinfection, surgical hand disinfection, thermometer disinfection
3. Usage Precautions:
   Alcohol-based disinfectants are flammable and should not be near open flames; after use, the lid should be tightly closed, sealed, and stored in a cool place; use with caution in allergic individuals; pour out for use, shelf life 7 days

<!-- Slide number: 24 -->
Alcohol-based Disinfectants

Hand Disinfection Gel: Composite preparation of ethanol and n-propanol
1. Main Ingredients: Ethanol 54-66%, n-propanol 9-11%, triclosan 0.04-0.06%
2. 500ml (for operating rooms) and 1L (for hand basins)
3. Uses:
     Surgical hand disinfection agent; rapid hand disinfection before aseptic operation in operating rooms
4. Notes:
    Thoroughly clean and dry hands before using hand disinfection gel; do not use if there are visible dirt or blood stains on the hands

Iodine-based Disinfectants
Types: 2% iodine tincture, Anruiod (3 types)

<!-- Slide number: 26 -->

2% Iodine Tincture
Active Ingredient: 2% iodine tincture
Uses: Skin disinfection in surgical fields, not for use on the skin of infants and young children, facial area, perineum, anal region, mucous membranes, etc.
3. Usage Precautions: The disinfection gauze should be moist but not dripping; it should not flow to other parts outside the surgical field; use 75% alcohol to deiodize 2 times or more after disinfection; contraindicated in iodine allergy

Third, Management Requirements for Sterile Items

         Sterile Items: Items that have been sterilized and not contaminated

<!-- Slide number: 32 -->
Transportation of Sterile Items
Should use a dedicated transportation cart, and after use, wipe with disinfectant and dry for standby.

Hands must be washed before contacting sterile items, maintain personal cleanliness and hygiene, and ensure that the transportation process of sterile items is not contaminated.

<!-- Slide number: 33 -->
Storage of Sterile Items

Storage Environment Requirements:
Temperature: 20-25 degrees; Humidity: 55-65%
Humidity and temperature indicator, use dehumidifier
Place in a clean area cabinet (or on shelves), the item cabinet is made of materials that are not easily absorbent and have a smooth surface, at least 50cm from the ceiling, at least 20-25cm from the ground, and at least 5cm from the wall, to reduce pollution from the ground, ceiling, and walls

<!-- Slide number: 34 -->
Management of Sterile Items
Managed by a designated person, check the qualification of sterile items daily. Check the item name, quantity, integrity of packaging, presence of sterilization identification, and expiration date before storage.
Sterile items are arranged in order of expiration date, items that are due to expire soon are used first
No expired items are allowed. Sterile items and non-sterile items are placed separately
High-pressure steam sterilized items and disposable sterile items are stored separately
Disposable sterile items should be unpacked before being placed in the storage room
