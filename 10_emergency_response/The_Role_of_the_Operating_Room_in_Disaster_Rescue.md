In the Asian region, floods, storms, and earthquakes account for 71% of all disasters.

<!-- Slide number: 3 -->
# Characteristics of Disasters
- Destructive
- Sudden
- Mass
- Complex

<!-- Slide number: 4 -->
In disasters, injuries are often severe, urgent, complex, numerous, and chaotic. The operating room serves as a crucial site for the rescue of injured patients.

<!-- Slide number: 5 -->
# Activation of Emergency Response Plan
- Establishment of rescue teams
- Effective communication
- Green channel activation
- Preparation of supplies
- Coordination of rescue efforts
- Infection control management

<!-- Slide number: 6 -->
# Rescue Team
- Hospital duty officer
- Anesthesia department chief
- Operating room nurse manager
- Strong in emergency response and first aid skills

<!-- Slide number: 7 -->
Green Channel
- Purpose: Smooth, efficient, and standardized
- Ensuring the availability of emergency personnel, supplies, and equipment
- Improving the success rate of critical patient rescue
- Highlighting the "urgent" characteristic to achieve the goal of "rescue"

# Preparation of Supplies
- Equipment
- Disposable items
- Emergency medications
- Instrumentation and equipment

<!-- Slide number: 10 -->
# Rescue Coordination
- Unity and assistance, division of labor and coordination.
- Strict implementation of aseptic techniques to reduce infection rates.
- Strict implementation of check-and-double procedures to prevent errors.
- Perfecting surgical and rescue records.

<!-- Slide number: 11 -->
# Infection Prevention and Control

- Personal protection
- Management of medical waste
- Strict implementation of disinfection and isolation procedures to prevent infection

<!-- Slide number: 12 -->
The operating room rescue is an important link in disaster rescue. Through long-term effective training and multi-specialty expertise, operating room nurses can maximize the role of limited nursing staff in disaster rescue.
